Nana's Playroom

Parent-and-toddler

Here at Nana's Playroom we offer Child-led, Messy, Sensory- based Play Sessions. There are a variety of carefully selected, open ended play materials that children are free to explore during their group time. The classes leave the creativity to the child! Remember there is no right or wrong way to play as long as you are having fun! This way we let our children's development thrive through imagination ❤️ We are based in Ballinlough Community Centre, Cork and run classes on a Sunday 9:30-10:30 am. We also run classes in Dramarama, Mount Oval, Rochestown, Cork. Parents or carers must supervise their children at all times. We encourage that parents or carers get involved in the chaos and take this time as a fun, messy bonding experience for adult and child. We advise to bring 1 spare outfit with you just incase your little one get a bit messy from water, paint etc.

Ages: 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Contact: nanasplayroomcork@gmail.com
Address: Ballinlough Comm Ctr, Ballintemple, Cork, Co. Cork, Ireland
