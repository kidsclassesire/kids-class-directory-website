Newtown Cork Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Newtownshandrum Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is a fun filled playgroup. Join for activities, music, play, buggy walking group and guest classes. There are snack and tea/coffee on offer. The group is open to Parents and All Care Givers.
