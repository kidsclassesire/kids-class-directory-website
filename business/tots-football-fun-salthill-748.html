Tots Football Fun - Salthill

Football

Our weekly training programme for children aged 18 months – 5 year olds offers an introduction to football through fun games and activities, inside our fantastic inflatable football pitch! Inside our exciting inflatable pitch, we create a fantastic environment where your child can enjoy taking their first steps into football. We offer a ‘free play’ opportunity on arrival and then deliver a range of fun games and activities to help them develop their football skills & life skills along the way. Our sessions take place at Salthill Knocknacarra GAA Club every Sunday morning at various session times.
