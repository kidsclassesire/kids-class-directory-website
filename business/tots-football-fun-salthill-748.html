Tots Football Fun - Salthill

Football

Our weekly training programme for children aged 18 months to 5 years offers an introduction to football through fun games and activities inside our inflatable football pitch, creating a fantastic environment where your child can take their first steps into football. We offer a 'free play' opportunity on arrival, then deliver a range of fun games and activities to help develop football skills and life skills along the way. Sessions take place at Salthill Knocknacarra GAA Club every Sunday morning, at various times: 9:30-10:15am for 18 months-3 years, and 10:15-11am for 3-5 years. Register for our free trial.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
