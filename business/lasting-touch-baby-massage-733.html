Lasting Touch Baby Massage

Developmental

Baby massage classes in Athlone, Ballinasloe and Craughwell, facilitated by a registered midwife/certified infant massage instructor. Ballinasloe classes: every Thursday, 18th June-16th July, 10-11.30am. Athlone classes: every Friday, 19th June-17th July, 10-11.30am. Craughwell classes: every Wednesday, 1st-29th July, 10-11.30am. Up to €100 redeemable with health insurers. Tea/coffee and a sweet treat provided - a lovely opportunity to bond with your baby and meet other mums in the area. Group classes are limited to one parent and one baby per booking. Private classes are available in your own home if additional parents or children would like to be present.
