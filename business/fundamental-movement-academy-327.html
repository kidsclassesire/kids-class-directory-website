Fundamental Movement Academy

Athletics

Fundamental Movement Academy (FMA) helps children develop the essential movement skills that form the foundation for all sports and physical activity. Fun, engaging sessions focus on running, jumping, throwing, catching, balance, coordination, agility, confidence and teamwork. Each 6-week block focuses on a different fundamental movement skill through games, challenges and age-appropriate activities. Every child receives an FMA Movement Journal to track their progress, complete weekly home challenges, and celebrate their achievements. Class times: ages 5-7 years, Wednesdays 4-4:50pm; ages 8-12 years, Wednesdays 5-5:50pm. Location: Louth, Dundalk. Open to children aged 5-12 years; parents/guardians are welcome to stay or return at the end of the session. Children should wear comfortable sports clothing and runners/trainers, and bring a bottle of water. Trial sessions available. Sibling discount available. Move Better. Play Better. Grow Better.
