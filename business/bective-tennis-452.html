Bective Tennis

Tennis

Bective Tennis Club is a family-friendly club welcoming children aged 4 and up, with a focus on fun, development, and inclusive participation. The junior programme includes after-school coaching throughout the year, holiday tennis camps, junior blitzes and supervised sessions, social tennis mixers and inter-club events, and junior championships and parent-child tournaments.

Address: Main Street, Donnybrook, Dublin, D04 A0P1, Ireland
