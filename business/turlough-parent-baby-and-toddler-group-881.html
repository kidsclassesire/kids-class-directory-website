Turlough Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Turlough Parent and Toddler Group Co Mayo is open to parents, grandparents and all care givers. The aim is to meet new people with kids of a similar age. Have a cuppa and a chat while the babies and toddlers play with some toys and engage with one another. A Public health nurse also attends if you have any questions on breastfeeding support,weaning, tips on tummy time/walking and much more. Keep up to date on our socials. 📍 Location: Turlough community centre, Turlough Rd, Leckneen, Castlebar, Co. Mayo ⏰Day/Time: Every Wednesday from 10.00 -11.30 (during school term) 💶 Cost: FREE per Family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact the public health nurse or join the facebook page for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Address: Turlough Rd, Castlebar, Co. Mayo, Ireland
