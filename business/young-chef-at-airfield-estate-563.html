Young Chef at Airfield Estate

Cooking

Airfield Estate’s Young Chef Workshops offer a hands-on cooking experience for budding chefs aged 8-12. Set in the beautiful Inspiration Kitchen, overlooking the estate’s food garden, kids will learn to create wholesome, seasonal meals using ingredients grown on-site whenever possible. | Led by experienced educators, these Sunday workshops (10 am – 12:30 pm) cover both sweet and savory dishes, helping young chefs develop practical cooking skills and a deeper connection to fresh, sustainable ingredients.
