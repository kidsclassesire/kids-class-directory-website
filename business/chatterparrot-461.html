Chatterparrot

Developmental

Chatterparrot was set up to provide babies, toddlers and their grown-ups with a friendly space to boost bonding and communication from birth, through baby sign language. The classes use a mix of songs, stories, sensory play and baby sign to focus on interaction and learning those early words. The class is run by Helen O'Sullivan, a qualified language teacher and linguist, passionate about bringing the joy of language to little ones and giving parents some precious play-time with their babies. There are classes in Rathmines, Kilternan and online - find your best class on the website.
