Chatterparrot

Developmental

Baby Sign Language | Chatterparrot was set up to provide babies, toddlers and their grown-ups with a friendly space to boost bonding and communication from birth. The classes use a mix of songs, stories, sensory play and baby sign to focus on interaction and learning those early words. The class is run by Helen O’Sullivan qualified language teacher and linguist, passionate about bringing the joy of language to little ones and giving parents some precious play-time with their babies. | There are classes in Rathmines, Kilternan and online. | Find your best class here.
