Tennis4Kids

Tennis

Tennis4Kids is a specially designed programme for children aged 5 to 12, helping kids of all abilities enjoy and learn tennis in a fun, supportive environment. Using smaller courts, softer balls, and junior-sized rackets, children can develop skills at their own pace while building confidence and coordination. The friendly coaching team gets to know each player, encouraging progress and a love for the game - perfect for beginners and experienced young players alike.
