Happy Peeps Sensory Play

Developmental

Location - Leixlip Youth & Community Centre Time - 10.30am-11.30am Children have the opportunity to regulate, engage in messy play and express themselves in our safe and supportive environment with smaller group sizes. Classes are child-led, relaxed, lots of fun and lots to explore. Caregivers such as parents, grandparents and childminders can attend. Caregivers are to stay for the duration of the class. Bring spare change of clothes, small towel / baby wipes. I also run Babies & Toddlers Sesnory Play in a different location, keep an eye on our Instagram page for location updates.
