Happy Peeps Sensory Play

Developmental

Location: Leixlip Youth & Community Centre. Time: 10.30-11.30am. Children have the opportunity to regulate, engage in messy play and express themselves in a safe and supportive environment with smaller group sizes. Classes are child-led, relaxed, and full of fun and things to explore. Caregivers such as parents, grandparents and childminders can attend and are required to stay for the duration of the class. Bring a spare change of clothes and a small towel/baby wipes. I also run Babies & Toddlers Sensory Play in a different location - keep an eye on our Instagram page for location updates, where you can also book, or contact Vicky on 0857377109 for any questions.

Ages: 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old
