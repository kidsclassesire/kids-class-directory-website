Phibsboro Toddler Storytime Group - Phibsboro Library

Parent-and-toddler

Phibsboro Library offers toddler storytime a great way to encourage early reading and early learning. They sometimes have special guests like Sarah Sparkles doing fun songs too. The library also puts out some toys for the kids to play with after the stories. 📍Location: Phibsboro Library, Blacquiere Bridge, Dublin 7 ⏰Time: Second Tuesday of the month at 11.30am. 💶 Cost: €FREE 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 01 222 8333 / phibsborolibrary@dublincity.ie for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old
Contact: 01 222 8333 · phibsborolibrary@dublincity.ie
