Baby Sensory Dunboyne

Developmental

Baby Sensory® classes are designed to support your baby’s development from birth to 13 months through gentle sensory activities, music, movement and play. Every activity is carefully designed using research into early childhood development, supporting your baby’s sensory, physical and cognitive growth in a natural, fun way. Each class is carefully structured for your baby’s stage, from tiny newborns to active explorers, all in a warm, welcoming environment. ⸻ Why parents love Baby Sensory: 💛 A special time to bond with your baby 💚 Supports early development and key milestones 💛 A friendly space to meet other local parents 💚 No pressure, just enjoy the moment together ⸻ What to expect: 💛 Sensory play (lights, textures, bubbles and more) 💚 Music and movement 💛 Varied, age-appropriate activities All designed to stimulate your baby’s senses while keeping things calm and enjoyable for you both.
