Surfdock

Watersports

Based at Grand Canal Dock, Surfdock offer a unique combination of activities including | paddleboarding, kayaking and windsurfing for beginners. They offer intermediate paddleboarding classes and trips in and around Dublin as well as intermediate windsurfing in the Malahide Estuary. | ​ | Their group sessions are popular for kids birthday parties from April to October. They also run a summer camp for 8 to 16 year olds, with an opportunity to try everything from kayaking, sailing, windsurfing, wakeboarding, paddleboarding and ringo rides. | ​ | In addition, they run SUP Yoga classes​ with Ireland’s only qualified SUP Yoga instructor Alexa von Zett of Dublin SUP Yoga Fitness and Urban Yoga Dublin.
