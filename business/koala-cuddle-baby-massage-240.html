Koala Cuddle Baby Massage

Developmental

Baby massage is a gorgeous time to spend some relaxed 1:1 time with your baby bonding. Learn how to massage your baby from head to toe and gain all the benefits of relief from colic/constipation/wind and teething. Massage promotes sleep and bonding. Enjoy a hot cuppa with other mums and share some tips and tricks and share all the ups n downs of parenthood. Certified infant massage instructor OT approved Tummy Time Practitioner Nurse and mum of 3 Classes take place at The Park Community Centre, Ballycragh D24 on a Tuesday morning Each term runs for 5 weeks lasting 1-1.5hrs each week. Classes are open for mums or dads to attend with their baby aged 3 weeks-pre crawling.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
