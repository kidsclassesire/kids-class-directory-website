Navan Library Parent and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Navan Parent & Toddler Group is hosted by Navan Library. Come along for stories and rhymes in this Parent and Toddler session every Wednesday at Navan Library. The story session is followed by free play with toys and games provided. It's a way to keep the little ones entertained while equipping them with early literacy skills and you can meet some parents for a chat too. 📍 Location: Navan Library, Railway St, Dillonsland, Navan, Co. Meath, C15 RW31 ⏰Day/Time: Every Wednesday 10.15am – 11.15am (Check during school term breaks as sometimes the group maybe cancelled to facilitate kids camps) 💶 Cost: Free 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 046 9021134 for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Contact: 046 9021134
