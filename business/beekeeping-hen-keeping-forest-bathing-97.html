Beekeeping, hen keeping, forest bathing

Camps

BeeWild for Kids offers an interactive beekeeping session covering equipment, hive-mind education, honey tastings, and sowing seeds to take home, plus the chance to meet the hens, pigs, calves and chicks and go bee-spotting; tea and organic treats are included. The BeeWild Combo adds a fuller beekeeping setup covering the hive as medicine, the matriarch bee, honey tastings, what trees to plant, weeds as wonders, and bee-spotting, alongside a chicken setup covering hatching and incubators, hygiene and health, the benefits of roosters, and meeting the chicks, and a grow-your-own setup covering weed suppression, natural fertiliser, pest prevention, and sowing seeds to take home; tea and organic treats are included. Forest Bathing sessions, held in the forest on site on the farm, cover trees as medicine, a plant ID walk, how to regulate your nervous system, benefits to your immune system, sensory exercises, breathing practices, guided visualisation, and a tea circle in the woods.
