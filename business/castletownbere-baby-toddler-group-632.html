Castletownbere Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Castletownbere Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is hosted by Beara West Resource Centre. Mothers, Fathers, Grandparents, Childminders and Au Pairs all very welcome to come along and join. There is a watsapp group to keep up to date with what is happening in the group. It is a perfect opportunity for baby and kids to play and meet other children. Also a great place for adults to have a chat and meet new people in the area. The class is pop in so why not pop along. 📍Location: Beara West Resource Centre, Main St, Castletownbere Cork, Ireland P75WN88 🗓️⏰Time: Every Thursday morning 10:15- 11:45 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €5 per family 📣 Contact Details: you can contact info@bearawestfrc.ie for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
