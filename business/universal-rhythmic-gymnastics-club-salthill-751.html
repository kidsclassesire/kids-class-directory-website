Universal Rhythmic Gymnastics Club -Salthill

Gymnastics

Join our Rhythmic Gymnastics Club and let your kid explore the fun of forward rolls, balancing, flexibility and improving coordination and body expression. We'll learn ribbon, ball, hoop, rope and clubs techniques, following the rhythm of the music in a fun environment. Sign up today and try a class. Small group sizes, and kids can join from age 4 to 14. Classes are one hour in duration. We're based in Salthill, Galway, Doctor Mannix Road (Salthill-Knocknacarra GAA Club).
