Aura Leisure

Swimming

Aura Leisure De Paul on Navan Road, Dublin 7, offers a comprehensive and engaging swimming programme for children aged 4 and up. Their Swim Academy features 10 progressive levels, starting from foundational water skills in the 'Ducklings' class to advanced stroke techniques and lifesaving skills in the 'Sharks' level. Each stage is designed to build confidence, safety awareness, and swimming proficiency in a fun and supportive environment. Lessons are conducted by professionally trained instructors in both the learner and main pools, ensuring appropriate challenges as children advance. For younger swimmers, the Junior Swim Academy provides 'Swim Baby' classes for infants up to 30 months and 'Swim Tots' classes for toddlers, focusing on water familiarity and parent-child bonding. With free swim assessments available to determine the appropriate starting level, Aura Leisure De Paul ensures every child receives personalised instruction tailored to their needs.
