Belmullet Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Belmullet Parent and Toddler Group Co Mayo is a group for parents and caregivers and their children aged 0-5years. Toys, snacks, tea & coffee provided! It is hosted by the Erris Family and Community Support Centre. The Erris Family and Community Support Centre at times also host baby massage classes at an affordable rate. Keep an eye on their socials to ensure you sign up in time as spaces do be limited. Keep up to date on our socials. 📍 Location: Irish Wheelchair Centre, 1 College Park, Logmore, Belmullet, Co.
