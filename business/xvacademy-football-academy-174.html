XVAcademy – Football Academy

Sports

XVAcademy Football Academy provides high-quality football coaching for children and young players of all abilities in a positive, professional, and enjoyable environment. Our sessions focus on developing technical skills, game intelligence, confidence, teamwork, and a lifelong love of football. Players are coached by experienced, qualified coaches using structured training sessions designed to help every player reach their potential, whether they are just starting out or looking to develop their game further. We offer football coaching, skills development, small group training, and seasonal camps, with an emphasis on player development, respect, hard work, and enjoyment. At XVAcademy, every player is encouraged to learn at their own pace while building confidence both on and off the pitch. We welcome boys and girls of varying experience levels and aim to create an inclusive, supportive environment where every child can thrive. Visit our website or social media pages to learn more about our programmes, locations, and how to get involved.
