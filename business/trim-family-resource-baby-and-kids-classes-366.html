Trim Family Resource - Baby and Kids Classes

Parent-and-toddler

Trim Family Resource Centre offers lots of great groups for Babies, Toddlers, Kids and Parents. **Parent and Toddler Group: For Toddlers of 1yrs old – 4yrs old to bring their grown up to. Learn how to share and play with other children, through songs, rhymes and play time. We enjoy nothing better than getting messy and making noise – and with the help of some enthusiastic facilitators we all get to learn new things and experience all that being a Toddler brings. Meeting in The Bungalow on Thursday mornings @10.45am **Babies and Bumps: A great place for expectant parents or parents of children up to 1 year to meet up. Every Thursday 9.45am-10.30 **Buggy Buddies: Buggy Buddies is not just for buggies but open to all, tall and small! If you have a little one and want to get out to enjoy a fun morning of walking, talking and laughing, pop down to our Buddy Buddies group, Tuesday mornings at 10:45am. Meeting on the benches Trim Castle Bridge Seats - across from Franzinis **Sibshops are fun and interactive workshops for children aged 8 -12 who have a sibling with additional care needs at home. Workshops include games, activities and healthy snacks. For further information or to book a place please email youngcarers@familycarers.ie **Cula Bula Youth Club is a volunteer led youth club for children aged 6 to 12 who usually meet one hour per week on a Monday evening. Sessions are planned with young people and in the past included art and crafts, baking, games, movie nights, annual trips away. For further information or to register, please contact culabulayouth@gmail.com **The Polish Children’s club is a volunteer led initiative open to all children in the community. The club aims to share the gift of Polish language and culture through fun activities, crafts, games, books and family events. **CoderDojo is a global movement of free, open, volunteer-led coding clubs (Dojos) where young people aged 7–17 (Ninjas) can explore digital technology with the support of their fellow Ninjas and volunteer mentors. CoderDojo’s mission is to give young people around the world the opportunity to learn to code in a social and safe environment. trim_frc.ie@coderdojo.com ** FREE PIANO classes are available to children aged 6 years and above. Lessons are taught by a qualified tutor. To book your child’s place contact us here at the FRC. Thanks to MAEDF grant from Louth and Meath ETB we are able to loan all equipment needed so children can access online classes from home. Nominal fee applies to loan of equipment to cover insurance and maintenance costs.
