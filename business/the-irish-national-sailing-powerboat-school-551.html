The Irish National Sailing & Powerboat School

Watersports

Based in Dun Laoghaire Harbour, The Irish National Sailing & Powerboat School has been teaching people on sailing courses and powerboating courses since 1978. They provide all essential gear, boats and buoyancy aids. Their highly experienced instructors are fully insured and have an excellent safety record.
