Toddle On

Parent-and-toddler

Welcome to Toddle On, where parents and children can bond in a warm and friendly environment. Toddle On play café is a warm, welcoming space where babies and young children can play, explore and learn while parents and carers can relax and connect. Play space is suitable for 0-10 year olds. We offer themed sensory and messy play classes for children aged 2 months to 6 years, designed to support early development through music, movement, storytelling and hands-on play. Classes are tailored by age group and include popular activities such as foam messy play, creative trays and imaginative role-play, all in a safe, nurturing environment. Our activities are thoughtfully crafted to provide your child with an enriching early learning experience while also fostering new friendships in the community. Come join us for a memorable time! Want to know more about the play space? Our play café consists of your rooms: 🌈 Sensory Play Room A calm, engaging space designed especially for babies and toddlers. This room features soft play, lights, textures and sensory toys that encourage early development, curiosity and gentle exploration in a relaxed environment. 🎭 Imaginative Play Room Perfect for little imaginations, this room allows children to role-play and create their own adventures. From pretend play to dressing up and themed setups, it supports social skills, confidence and creative thinking. A comfortable, welcoming area for parents and carers to relax while keeping an eye on their little ones. Enjoy a coffee, chat with other parents and take a breather while your children play safely nearby. 🎨 Creative & Messy Play Classroom A hands-on space where children are encouraged to explore, create and get messy. This room supports fine motor skills and learning through play — without the mess at home! 🎯Games Room The Games Room offers older children a dedicated space to enjoy a mix of active games and imaginative play. From basketball, mini snooker and football to creative play with Barbie, Paw Patrol, superheroes, puppets and dress-up, this room keeps older siblings happily engaged. Our Classes Toddle On Teenies Nurture your bond with your baby through our Toddle On Teenies program. With baby sensory activities, baby beats, lámh and baby massage, you and your little one will create lasting memories while supporting your baby's development. Toddle On Tinies Bond with your growing baby at Toddle On with our Toddle On Tinies program. Explore the world of baby sensory, lámh, messy play and music, using instruments to engage with your little one and bond over a weekly theme. Create lasting memories with your baby at Toddle On. Toddle On Tots Let your tot unleash their artistic imagination at Toddle On with our Toddle on Tots program. Each week, your child will delve into a new theme while learning a new sound, exploring messy play, role play, stories, numeracy, lámh, music, and crafts. Watch your tot develop and grow while having fun in a welcoming and engaging environment. Based in DB fitness and injuries, Unit 5 Pinewood Business Park (beside Lidl in Clonard).
