Dublin 2 Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Dublin 2 Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is a non-Profit Parent and Toddler Playgroup also known as Caterpillar Kidz Playgroup. The group is open to children 0-3 years. Our mission is to create a welcoming safe environment for parents, grandparents and carers to come and play with their children. Caterpillar Kidz aims to encourage children to socially interact with other children and grow and develop trough mutual play. We also hope the adults can relax and have a cup of tea and chat. 📍Location: 18a Adelaide Road (Presbyterian church) Dublin 2, Ireland ⏰Time: Every Tuesday 10.00 -11.30 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €FREE 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 0851200671 for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it may not align with term time.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old
