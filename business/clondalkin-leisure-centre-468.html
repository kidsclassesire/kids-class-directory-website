Clondalkin Leisure Centre

Swimming

Clondalkin Leisure Centre offers a comprehensive range of children's swimming lessons designed to build confidence, safety, and skill in the water. Starting from 3.5 years old, their structured programmes cater to all levels, from beginners to advanced swimmers and junior lifesavers. Led by certified instructors in a modern, award-winning facility, each lesson focuses on fun, progression, and water safety. With classes available every day, it's an ideal choice for families in Dublin 22 seeking quality swim instruction in a supportive environment.
