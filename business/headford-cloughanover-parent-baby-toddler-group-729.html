Headford/ Cloughanover Parent Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Headford/ Cloughanover Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Galway. Is open to mums, dads, grandparents, carers to pop along with their babies and toddlers. Lots of toys for the kids to play with while the adults enjoy a tea/coffee. The group also has a whatsapp group to stay up to date. 📍Location: Cloughanover Community Hall, Headford Road, Galway 🗓️⏰Time: Every Wednesday 10.00 - 12.00 (during school term) 💶 Cost: between €4- €8 🧸Contact Details: you can contact josephine.chini@icloud.com for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it may not align with term time.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: josephine.chini@icloud.com
Address: Headford Road, Galway, Ireland
