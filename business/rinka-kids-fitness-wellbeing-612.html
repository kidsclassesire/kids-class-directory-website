RINKA Kids Fitness & Wellbeing

Athletics

At RINKA we are on a mission to get kids moving in a non-competitive and, most importantly, fun environment. A fitness class like no other - your kids can expect exercise and dance routines, high-energy games, athletics like obstacle courses and relays, gymnastics, yoga, relaxation and mindfulness, and much more. No two classes are the same, and we pride ourselves on providing a fitness alternative to organised sports, while equally helping the development of sporty kids. RINKA Kids Fitness is a one-stop shop for your child's health, social and leisure needs. Camps, parties and schools also catered for.
