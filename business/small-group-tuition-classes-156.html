Small Group Tuition Classes

Camps

Guided Growth Academy is a small Irish-owned tuition business run by qualified primary school teachers. We cater for children of all primary school ages, from 5 to 12 years, from a variety of backgrounds, including children with additional needs. Services include: Social Skills Class, supporting children with confidence building, friendship skills, emotional regulation and teamwork; Easter/Summer Camps, combining fun academic learning with arts & crafts and outdoor activities; and Events, run for both children and their parents for fun and learning together.
