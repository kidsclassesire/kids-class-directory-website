Bravehearts Halloween Camp at Trinity Sports Centre, Dublin city centre

Sports

Flexible day bookings now available for €50 per day. Join us this mid-term break for an action-packed Bravehearts Halloween Camp 2025 at Trinity Sport! Running from Tuesday 28 October to Friday 31 October, the camp offers a full day of fun from 9am to 4pm, with pre-care (from 8am) and post-care (until 5pm) options available. Children aged 4-14 years will enjoy a wide range of exciting activities, from swimming, rugby and climbing to arts & crafts, dance and nature walks, all led by friendly, qualified coaches. Prices start from €170 for the week, or €50 for day bookings, with flexible care options and sibling discounts available. Spaces are limited, so secure your spot now through the SportsKey booking platform at https://portal.sportskey.com/venues/trinity-college-santry-sports-grounds/events. For any queries, contact children.sports@tcd.ie or call reception on 01 896 1812.
