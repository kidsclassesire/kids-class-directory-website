Belturbet Parent, Toddler & Baby Group

Parent-and-toddler

Belturbet Parent, Toddler and Baby Group in Cavan welcomes you to just come along & meet other parents/guardians & enjoy a cuppa while your toddler gets to play & socialise. There are mats and toys for the kids and tea/coffee for the adults. The library also offers a great sensory area for kids too. There is no need to book as these are pop in sessions. 📍 Location: Belturbet Library (Centrally located in the Townhall) The Diamond, Corporation Lands, Co. Cavan ⏰Time: Every Thursday 1pm -3 pm (during term time) 💶 Cost: €2 per Family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 049 952 2683 for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it does not always align with term time.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
