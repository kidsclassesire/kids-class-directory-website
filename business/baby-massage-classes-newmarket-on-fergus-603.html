Baby Massage Classes Newmarket on Fergus

Developmental

Baby massage is a way of communicating with your baby through a nurturing and loving approach. Infant massage is a gentle medium, following a simple massage routine that's adaptable to each family's own needs and wants. In my infant massage classes, your CIMI (instructor) demonstrates the massage movements on a doll while you massage your own baby, guided step by step in a relaxed and friendly environment, so you can continue to massage your infant for years to come. Babies who are massaged tend to sleep better and get relief from growing pains, muscular tension, teething discomfort, gas and colic. It also increases relaxation and reduces stress levels by stimulating the anti-stress hormones oxytocin and serotonin, for both parent and infant - a wonderful way to spend time with your baby. Classes run over 5 weeks. Stay up to date with new terms through our socials. Location: Harmony House, Newmarket-on-Fergus. To secure a place, contact Siobhan on 086 855 2307. With nearly a decade of teaching baby massage, you can be confident you're in safe hands.
