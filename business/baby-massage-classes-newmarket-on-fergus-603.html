Baby Massage Classes Newmarket on Fergus

Developmental

Baby Massage is a form of communicating with your baby through a nurturing and loving way. Infant massage is a gentle medium and follows a simple massage routine which is adaptable to each families own needs and wants. In my Infant massage classes, your CIMI (instructor) demonstrates the massage movements on a doll whilst you massage your own baby. You are guided step by step in a very relaxed and friendly environment and you can continue to massage your infant for years to come. Babies who are massaged tend to: sleep better, get relief from growing pains, muscular tension, teething discomfort, gas and colic. It also increases relaxation and reduces stress levels by stimulating anti-stress hormones, oxytocin and serotonin for both parents and infant. What a wonderful thing to do and way to spend time with your baby!!
