Dizzy Footwork (Dance, Drama & Acrobatic)

Dance

Dizzy Footwork Dance Academy offers exciting, professional classes in Drama, Lyrical Dance, Hip Hop Dance, and Acrobatics for children and teenagers of all ages and abilities. Our academy is led by highly experienced, passionate staff dedicated to helping every student grow in confidence, creativity, performance, fitness, and teamwork within a fun and supportive environment. Our classes are designed for different age groups, allowing students to learn alongside others their own age while developing new skills, friendships, and self-expression.
