Tipperary Town Toddler Group & Breastfeeding Group

Parent-and-toddler

Tipperary Town Baby and Toddler Group & Breastfeeding Support Group is hosted by three Drives Family Resource Centre. Parent and Toddler Group meet every week, it is the perfect way to connect with others in your community, chat with others and share tips with parents and carers’ alike. Everyone is encouraged to join in the fun and games! All are welcome. For mums who are breastfeeding, who have new babies or anyone thinking about breastfeeding. This group meets once a week and offers peer support on all topics related to breastfeeding support and practices. Babies and young toddlers will have the opportunity to connect with each other through play. 📍Location: 22/23 Greenane Drive, Tipperary Town, Co. Tipperary.
