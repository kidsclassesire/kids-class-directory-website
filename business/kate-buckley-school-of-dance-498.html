Kate Buckley School of Dance

Dance

The Kate Buckey School Of Dance provides | Ballet, Modern Jazz, Hip-Hop & Tapdance classes for ages 3 years old to young adults in | Rathfarnham, Dundrum and Knocklyon. Classes are suitable for boys and girls of all abilities and levels. Their aim is to give pupils a love of dance. | Pupils take part in shows/displays/youtube videos and can take RAD and ISTD Dance Examinations | throughout the year which gives students a goal and improves Pupils technically, artistically and gives them confidence.

Address: Dundrum, Knocklyon, Rathfarnham
