Language Explorers Workshops (For families with children 3 to 6 years old)

Developmental

Language Explorers is Mother Tongues' programme, designed for children aged 3 to 6 and their families. Facilitated by our specially trained multilingual artists, the Language Explorers workshops create a welcoming space for families from all linguistic and cultural backgrounds. Each session invites children and their adults to connect with their language(s) through storytelling, play, visual arts, music and movement. Our artists create a space full of imagination and creativity, in which speaking and listening to languages is fun and something to be proud of. Families take part in high-quality artistic activities while sharing and exploring the diversity of languages in the room, inspiring multilingual families and those curious about other languages and cultures. Workshops are delivered in English, and families are warmly encouraged to bring any and all languages they speak. Whether your child is fluent, just starting to learn, non-speaking, or communicates in a different way, this space is for you.
