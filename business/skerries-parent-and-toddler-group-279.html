Skerries Parent and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Skerries Parent and Toddler Group Join us for a fun-filled morning of play, crafts, and social time for both parents and toddlers. It's a great chance to meet other families in the community, let the little ones make friends, and enjoy some quality time together. New faces are always welcome 😊 📍 Location: Skerries Community Centre Day/Time: Every Wednesday during School Terms at 10:00 AM – 11:30 AM Cost: €5

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Skerries, County Dublin, Ireland
