Stillorgan Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Stillorgan Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is also known as the Honey BeeHive Playgroup. The perfect morning out of the house to meet other mums, dads and caregivers with their babies, toddlers/preschoolers. Lots of toys on offer and enjoy a coffee/tea and a gorgeous homemade treat. The group operates in a drop basis so no need to sign up for a term. 📍Location: St Brigid’s Parish Centre, Stillorgan 🗓️⏰Time: Every Tuesday 9.30am-11am (During school term time) 💶 Cost: €5 per family (Cash or Revelout on the day) 🧸Contact Details: you can contact the group through their private Instagram account or join the WhatsApp group for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: 0868567329
Address: Stillorgan, Dublin, Ireland
