Dun Laoghaire School of Music

Music

The school offers expert tuition in most instruments, theory and voice by experienced and dedicated teachers, providing individual or group classes for all ages and levels. A diverse range of musical styles is on offer, from classical to jazz through to popular and traditional music. They also run a The Children’s World of Music special awareness class for children aged 3 – 7 years of age.
