Monkstown Lawn Tennis Club

Sports

Junior tennis coaching for club members of all ages and standards at Ireland's oldest tennis club, running group coaching seven days a week during the school term with individual coaching also available.

Verified 2026-07-18
Schedule: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday · School-term programme
Ages: Juniors of all ages
Supervision: Varies by Age or Class
Junior coaching is available to club members only; a one-off joining fee applies to new members and there is sometimes a waiting list. Group coaching runs September to June; specific coaching fees were not published on the club website.
