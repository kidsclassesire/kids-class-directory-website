Donabate/Portrane Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Donabate/Portrane Parent and Toddler Group is open to parents/childminders and babies to pre-school children. They have an area for babies with playmats, bouncers, toys and an area for toddlers with slide, trampoline, balance beam, ball pit and lots of ride on toys. There is also a Sing-Song and Fruit break provided for the children. Why not pop along and get to meet some new people. 📍Location: Donabate Portrane Community Centre (Upstairs), Leisure Centre, 3 Portrane Rd, Donabate, Co. Dublin, Fingal ⏰Time: Every Monday from 10.15 - 12.00 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €3 per parent 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 086-3788075 for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it may not always align with term time.
