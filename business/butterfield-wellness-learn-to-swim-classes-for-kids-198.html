Butterfield Wellness Learn to Swim Classes for Kids

Swimming

Our kids' classes are perfect for complete beginners to improvers. Classes are small (8:1 teaching ratio) and taught by experienced teachers, ideal for little ones new to classes or looking to gain more confidence. We teach using fun activities, building confidence to help them learn to love the water and develop into competent, happy little swimmers. We teach from age 4 up. Weekly 30-minute classes, 10 weeks at €130, with a 5% sibling discount. To register your interest, visit our website. Camps are also available.
