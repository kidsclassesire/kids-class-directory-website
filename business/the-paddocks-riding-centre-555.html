The Paddocks Riding Centre

Horse Riding

The Paddocks Riding Centre, located high in the Dublin Mountains, is a family run horse riding centre with stables and facilities to house a large number of horses. Get out of the city and enjoy some riding lessons, or a one/two hour trek through the Dublin mountains enjoying the amazing views and beautiful scenery. They also host birthday parties.

Contact: 087 223 7981
Address: Woodside Road, Sandyford, Co. Dublin
