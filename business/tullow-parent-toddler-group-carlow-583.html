Tullow Parent & Toddler Group Carlow

Parent-and-toddler

Tullow Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Carlow is hosted by Forward Steps Family Resource Centre. Activities such as sensory play, crafts and free play time will ensure the kids have a good morning. It is a great way for parents to meet other parents in the area. To attend you must email in advance if you would like to attend as space is limited. Every Parent/Guardian must supervise their own child 📍Location: Forward Steps Family Resource Centre, Chapel Lane, Tullow, Carlow, Ireland 🗓️⏰Time: Every Wednesday from 10.00 - 11.30 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €2 per family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact receptionforwardsteps@gmail.com for any queries Note: Website may be out of date, keep up to date on dates/time on Facebook or by Contacting the centre.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
