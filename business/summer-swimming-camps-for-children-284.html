Summer Swimming Camps for Children

Swimming

SwimCamp is offers intensive swimming camps for children throughout the year at Castle Park School Swimming Pool in Dalkey, Dublin. These camps are designed for kids aged 5 and up who have some previous swimming experience. The classes usually lasting 45 minutes each day. The camps are divided into three skill levels (Group 1, 2, and 3) to accommodate different abilities, from beginners to more advanced swimmers. There are also special clinics for teens aged 13+. Children can be accompanied by any caregiver - parents, grandparents, or childminders. However, the website doesn't specify whether caregivers are required to stay for the duration of the class. It's best to contact SwimCamp directly for clarification on this point. Regarding what to bring or wear, the website doesn't provide specific information. Typically for swim classes, children should bring: A well-fitting swimsuit A towel Goggles (if preferred) A swim cap (if required by the pool) It's advisable to contact SwimCamp for any specific requirements or recommendations they might have for their classes. Each camp consists of three 45-minute sessions held on consecutive days. The camps focus on improving existing swimming skills, building confidence in the water, and teaching water safety. Experienced, certified coaches lead small groups to ensure personalized attention. SwimCamp emphasizes a fun and engaging learning environment while helping children develop essential swimming strokes, strength, and endurance. The camps are priced at €65 for the three-day session. Parents interested in enrolling their children can choose from multiple dates and time slots throughout July and August.
