Longford Town Toddler Group - Community Resource

Parent-and-toddler

Longford Parent and Toddler Group also known as Humpty Dumpty Parent and Toddler Group This is a group for parents of children not yet in preschool or national school. It is a space for mums,dads and care givers to come together to chat, share experiences and to get to know other parents in your area. 📍 Location: Longford Community Resource , Templemichael, Longford Town N39 RH22 ⏰ Day/Time: Every Tuesday morning 10.30am 💶 Cost: Free 📣 Contact Details: you can contact 087 945 1771 for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
