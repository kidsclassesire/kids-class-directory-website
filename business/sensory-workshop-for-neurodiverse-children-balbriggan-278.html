Sensory Workshop for Neurodiverse Children - Balbriggan

Developmental

Sensory Workshop for Neurodiverse Children: a fun, engaging session providing a safe space for children to explore sensory activities in a supportive environment. Max 8 children per session. Age: 4-10 years. Location: Balbriggan Library, George's Square, Balbriggan, Co. Dublin. Time: runs twice a month, 4-4.45pm. Cost: free. How to book: bookings open at the beginning of each month - email balbrigganlibrary@fingal.ie to secure a spot, and join the email list to be notified when bookings go live, as it's the first 8 each month. Important: parents/guardians must remain in the library during the session - you don't need to stay in the room, but must stay on the premises. Let's create a fun, inclusive space for all children.

Ages: 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old
Contact: balbrigganlibrary@fingal.ie
Address: Saint George's Square, Balbriggan, County Dublin, Ireland
