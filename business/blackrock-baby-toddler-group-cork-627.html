Blackrock Baby & Toddler Group Cork

Parent-and-toddler

Blackrock/Ballintemple Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is a lovely group to meet other mums, dads, grandparents and care givers. The group has special guests/classes throughout the year. Your babies and toddler will enjoy the group too! There are plenty of toys to play with and a chance to interact with other kids to make friends. Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
