Donnybrook Lawn

Tennis

Donnybrook Lawn Tennis Club offers a welcoming and inclusive environment where children of all ages and skill levels can enjoy and improve their tennis in a fun, relaxed setting. The Junior Programme supports both social and competitive play through regular events like Super Saturdays, Round Robin tournaments, Play & Pizza nights, and inter-club matches. Additional sessions are organised as needed, including match practice ahead of tournaments or league play. During school breaks, Donnybrook runs junior tennis camps in February, Easter, and summer. The Junior Club Championships in late August are a highlight of the year, giving young players a chance to compete and celebrate their progress.
