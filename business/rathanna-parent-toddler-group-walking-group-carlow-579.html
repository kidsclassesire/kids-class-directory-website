Rathanna Parent & Toddler Group/ Walking Group Carlow

Parent-and-toddler

Rathanna Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Carlow is a weekly playgroup for parents/ caregivers, grandparents/childminders and their little ones from newborn to preschool. What to expect ?? You may arrive & leave anytime during playgroup session and typically everyone helps to tidy up for the last 5-10mins of the session & there are changing facilities available. We are located in one big hall that are filled with toys appropriate for 0-5 years and include small ride on cars/ tractors, prams, Lego, trains, ball pit, reading corner and soft play area on large soft mats to protect the little ones. The group also has a parents walking group which operates at certain times throughout the year. Walk the gorgeous Rathanna Sli na Slainte route (5.4km) - check out their socials to see if the group is operating.
