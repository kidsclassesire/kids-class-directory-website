Tallaght Parent, Baby and Toddler Group -St Kevin's Family Resource Centre

Parent-and-toddler

Tallaght Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is hosted by St Kevin's Family Resource Centre. The group is open to all families/carers and minders of young children. There is free play for the children, songs, arts and crafts and refreshments to enjoy while getting to meet new people. The classes are pop in so don't worry if you are running late due to that nappy change. The group is in demand so you must join the waiting list. Day/Time: Every Tuesday during Term Time 10.30-12.30 Cost: €3 per family ** Infant Massage The Family Resource Centre offers Baby Massage Classes on occasions which are in demand due to their price. Keep an eye on their socials to sign up for these classes. Highly recommended. Location: St. Kevin's Family Resource Centre, St. Kevin's Girls National School, Treepark Road, Kilnamanagh, Tallaght, Dublin 24, D24 R6PT.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: info@stkevinsfrc.ie
Address: Kilnamanagh, Dublin, D24 R6PT, Ireland
