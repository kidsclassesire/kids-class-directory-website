Stratford Lawn

Tennis

Stratford LTC offers year-round junior coaching on four floodlit all-weather courts, with access to four grass courts during summer. Coaching is available for children aged 5 and up, with lessons grouped by age and skill level. Classes follow the school year in 6-week blocks, focusing on technique, drills, and gameplay. Each 60-minute session uses the ITF Tennis 10s system, with red, orange, and green balls to support skill development. Summer camps replace weekly lessons during school holidays. All coaches are experienced, Garda vetted, and trained in child safeguarding.

Address: Stratford Lawn Tennis Club, Grosvenor Square, Rathmines, Dublin 6
