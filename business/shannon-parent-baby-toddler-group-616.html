Shannon Parent, Baby & Toddler Group in Clare Welcomes all parents/grandparents/guardians & child-minders and their tots up to 3 years old. "We’ve lots of space; a great cup of tea or coffee (and toast), and a wide range of activities on offer; from our Ark soft-play area, painting and play-dough, big blocks, balloon-art & baby-zone and to our lil kidz disco-dancing." Please book in advance to secure a place 📍 Location: Hope Cafè Shannon, Unit 2, Main Road, Smithstown, Shannon, Ireland V14 KX52 ⏰ Day/Time: Every Tuesday and Thursday mornings from 10-11:30am (during term time) 💶 Cost: €3 for 1-3 years or €1 for under 1's 📣 Contact Details: you can contact Elton on 086 300 9450. for any queries
