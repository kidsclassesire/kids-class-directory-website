Kinvara Mum to-Be & New Mum Social Group

Parent-and-toddler

Kinvara Mum to Be and New Mum Social Group in Galway This group is a welcoming space for soon to be mums and new mothers to come and get peer led support and advice during the first year of motherhood. Enjoy a tea and cake while meeting new parents that are experiencing the same tiredness and joy that parenthood can bring. A perfect place to build new friendships. There is a whatsapp group you can join too. 📍Location: SIAR cafe, Kinvara, Dungory West, The Quay, Kinvara, Galway, Ireland 🗓️⏰Time: Every Second Tuesday from 9.30 - 11.00am (Sign up to their whatapp group for upcoming meetings) 💶 Cost: FREE 🧸Contact Details: you can contact the group through their Facebook Page for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old
Address: Cartron, Kinvarra, County Galway, H91 P6PP, Ireland
