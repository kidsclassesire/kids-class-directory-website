Kill Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Kildare Kill Parent, Baby and Toddler Group also known as Kill Time Playgroup. A time to meet new friends, let your little ones play while you enjoy some tea/coffee and some sneaky treats 😀 It’s such a lovely space for the kids to play and for parents to have a chat and get out of the house. It's pay as you go so why not group in? The group has a watsapp group and run special events at times such as sensory classes with Little Turtles. During the summer they operate a buggy walking group too. Location: Hope Cottage (across from Spar) Main St, Kill West, Kill, Co. Kildare Day/Time: Every Monday from 10 till 12 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €3 per family

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Main St, Kill West, Kill, Co. Kildare, Ireland
