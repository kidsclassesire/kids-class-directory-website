Carndonagh Inishowen Toddler Group and More - Spraoi agus Sport

Developmental

Spraoi Agus Sport offers a range of facilities to families in Carndonagh, Inishowen, Co. Donegal. The Parent and Toddler Group runs multiple times a week, perfect to work around schedules - parents and their young children are encouraged to come along to this fun and relaxed 3-hour session, with a multitude of toys available for the children while mum, dad or carer gets a chance to have a well-deserved cuppa. Location: Ray Community Centre, Co. Donegal, F92 CD70. Monday, 11am-1pm (term time); Tuesday, 10am-1pm; Thursday, 10am-1pm (term time); Saturday, 10am-1pm (term time). Cost: €5 per family, bookable and payable online for one or multiple sessions. Contact: info@spraoiagussport.ie. Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays, as it may not always align with term time. Classes and groups for babies to preschoolers include music parent and toddler classes, baby massage, and breastfeeding support. Spraoi agus Spórt also supports children with a disability and/or autism, along with their parents and siblings, hosting quarterly meet-ups for parents and encouraging them to attend parent and toddler groups and classes with their child - a fully resourced sensory room is available for families to use during the week or at weekends. Parent workshops cover topics including weaning, behaviour management, potty training, and paediatric first aid. Classes and groups for kids aged 5-15 include creative writing, sewing/crochet, guitar lessons, and gaming.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: info@spraoiagussport.ie
Address: Churchland Quarters(Carrowtemple Moneyshandoney And Carrick), County Donegal, F93 AY6D, Ireland
