Carndonagh Inishowen Toddler Group and More - Spraoi agus Sport

Developmental

Spraoi Agus Sport offer a range of facilities to families in Carndonagh, Inishowen, Co. Donegal. ** Parent and Toddler Group is on multiple times a week so perfect to work around work schedules. Parents and their young children are encouraged to come along to this fun and relaxed 3 hour session, a multitude of toys are available for the children to play with while Mum or Dad or Carer get a chance to have a well-deserved cuppa! 📍 Location: Ray Community Centre, County Donegal, F92 CD70, Ireland ⏰Monday 11am-1pm (Term time) ⏰Tuesday 10am-1pm ⏰Thursday 10am-1pm (Term time) ⏰Saturday 10am-1pm (Term time) 💶 Cost: €5 per Family and you can book and pay online for one or multiple sessions 🧸Contact Details: you can contact info@spraoiagussport.ie for any queries ---Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it may not always align with term time. ** Classes and Groups for Babies to Preschoolers A wide range for babies including Music parent and toddler classes, Baby Massage, breastfeeding support ** Special Needs Spraoi agus Spórt support children with a disability and/or autism, their parents, and siblings. They host quarterly meet ups for parents and encourage parents to attend parent and toddler groups and classes with their child. They have a fully resourced sensory room which can be used by families during the week or at weekends. ** Parent Workshops The offer a range of parental support workshops including: Weaning, Behaviour Management, Potty Training, Paediatric First Aid. ** Classes and Groups for Kids A wide range for kids ages 5-15 years including Creative Writing, Sewing/Crochet, Guitar Lessons, Gaming

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
