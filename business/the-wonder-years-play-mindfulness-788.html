The Wonder Years Play & Mindfulness

Developmental

Join The Wonder Years Play & Mindfulness for a four-week block of messy play classes, suitable for kids aged 9 months to 4 years old. You can find us in The Scouts Den in Kilkenny. Classes are term-based and usually run on Friday, 10.30-11.30am. Stay up to date through our social media.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
