Toddle Sense

Developmental

Classes range from 13 months to 4.5 years. From Space to Under the Sea, to an Ugly Bug Ball and a trek in the Jungle, Toddler Sense explores it all. All activities are designed to work on a range of skills and developmental milestones, including hand-eye coordination, gross motor, fine motor, visual stimulation, emotional regulation, exploring the world around us, and social and emotional development, to name a few. A twin/sibling discount is available for all classes, and Baby Sensory graduates can avail of a discount when booking a class within the first 12 months of graduation. Multiple locations across Dublin: Balgriffin Hall, Malahide Road, Dublin; Fingallians GAA Club, Seatown West, Swords, Dublin; Gracepark Educate Together, DCU Campus, All Hallows, D09 KDW4, Dublin.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
