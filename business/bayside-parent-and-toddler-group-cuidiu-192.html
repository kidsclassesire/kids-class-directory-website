Bayside Parent and Toddler Group - Cuidiu

Parent-and-toddler

Bayside Parent and Toddler Group is conveniently located in Bayside Community. Perfect for all parents, childmiders or caregivers. The perfect way for your little one have a bit of free play fun and practice those action songs. While you can enjoy a tea/coffee and some biscuits Location: Bayside Community Centre. Day/Time: Every Thursday during School Terms at 9.30 to 11.30 am. Cost: €3 for Cuidiú Members / €5 for non members Cuidiú is a charity and as such runs off donations. You can become a Cuidiú Member for €25 per year (plus PayPal fees) or half year membership is just €13. Membership includes: * Discount on the weekly parent and toddler groups and other Cuidiu activity and events * Discounts in a wide range of shops/businesses. * After 6 months you can apply for training sessions including Antenatal Teaching, Breastfeeding Counsellor, Parent Supporter Training (A good option if you want to train up in something new and have a side job or switch careers).

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Bayside, Dublin, Ireland
