Limerick Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Limerick Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Limerick is also known as Park and Playgroup. One of the longest running toddler playgroups in Limerick City. The group has a warm and welcoming atmosphere. Meet other parents,grandparents or caregivers with their little ones. The group hosts festive and seasonal events/activities throughout the year so be sure to check them out. The group operates in a drop in format so no need to sign up for a term. 📍Location: St Michael's Church, Barrington Street, Limerick, Ireland 🗓️⏰Time: Every Thursday morning from 10am-12 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €2 per family (donation) 📣 Contact Details: you can contact 086 849 9521 for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
