Galway Community Circus

Developmental

Galway Community Circus was created to meet the needs of our local youth by offering a non-competitive, physical and creative activity in a safe and welcoming space. Our philosophy is 'circus for all', and we use the circus arts to teach young people, including those who are disadvantaged, at-risk and vulnerable, the tools to develop self-confidence, resilience and social skills while tapping into strengths and hidden talents. Circus classes offer something for everyone to enjoy and are suitable for all ages, levels and abilities. We offer multiple classes for ages 1-17 years (and adults 18+ too). Our main location is in Shantalla on Galway's Westside, but we also offer classes in Renmore on Galway's Eastside, as well as in Tuam. We offer Toddler Circus classes for ages 1-4 years with an adult on Mondays, Thursdays and Saturdays in Shantalla, and on Fridays in Renmore - adults are welcome to participate but not required, though they must stay in the class. We offer Family Circus classes in Shantalla on Saturdays for ages 4+ with siblings who can be younger or older; adults are expected to participate in this class, a fun activity for the whole family. We also offer tea, coffee and social time before and after our Toddler and Family Circus classes. Our Youth Circus programme features weekly classes for different age groups: 5-7 years, 8-11 years, and 12-17 years - in these classes, parents/caregivers are not allowed to stay. At our Shantalla location, we offer youth circus classes for ages 1-17 years; at Renmore, ages 1-12 years; and at Tuam, ages 5-12 years. All classes run alongside the school year from September to June, on different days depending on the class, and we also offer week-long summer camps by age group. Participants should wear comfortable clothes, bring a water bottle, and wear grippy socks if they have them. Classes vary in price based on age group and length, and for 9 months of classes, fees are paid via a monthly subscription; some classes offer trials. Check out all our classes at https://www.galwaycommunitycircus.com/
