Portarlington Toddler Group & Buggy Group

Parent-and-toddler

Portarlington Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Laois Activities on a monthly basis which is available to view on our social media accounts (facebook & Instagram) and also on our Members WhatsApp group. Keep an eye out for any special events that we are planning throughout the year! A light healthy snack is provided e.g. crackers/ toast with ham, cheese etc for both adults and also children. Refreshments of hot drinks are also available for all adults attending and we have water and dilute juice available for the children. Classes are limited to 35 children per class so it is essential to pre-book your class in advance, as we wouldn’t like to be turning people away! ** Membership of the Group offers a discounted class rate, priority refusal on activities, and a loyalty programme.
