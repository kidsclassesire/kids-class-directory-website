New Ross Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Wexford New Ross parent and toddler group is an opportunity for parents to meet for a cup of tea/coffee and a chat while the children get to play. If you would like to attend, please do come along. Registration or sign up is not required prior to attending. 📍 Location: Youth New Ross, The Bullawn, New Ross, Co.
