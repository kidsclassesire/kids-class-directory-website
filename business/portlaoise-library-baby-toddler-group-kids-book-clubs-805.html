Portlaoise Library - Baby & Toddler Group / Kids Book Clubs

Parent-and-toddler

Portlaoise Library has a great selection of groups for families. Listed below are some of the regular events. During school holidays the library runs many pop up classes/workshops which are definitely worth checking out. Sign up to their newsletter to stay up to date. 📍Location: Portlaoise Library, 23 Lower Main Street, Portlaoise, Co.
