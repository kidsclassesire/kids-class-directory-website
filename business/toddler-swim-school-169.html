Toddler Swim School

Swimming

Our toddler swimming lessons are for kids aged 1 yr to 4 yrs old. We have great success with teaching young kids a brilliant life skill through our fun swimming classes, growing their confidence in the water. We help kids to develop their swimming skills at a pace that suits them with the aim of having them swimming independently before they begin primary school but many achieve this way in advance of that thanks to our fun and encouraging approach.
