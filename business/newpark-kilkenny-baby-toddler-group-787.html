Newpark Kilkenny - Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

New Park Parent and Toddler Group is based in Kilkenny. This group is running over 25 years!! Parents and Childminders enjoy a chat, cup of Tea or Coffee while their children play in a safe relaxed environment. There are toys galore, story time, music time and of course snack time.
