Balance Ireland

Sports

Aikido is a dynamic martial art that combines practical self-defense techniques with a focus on building confidence and agility. Kids aged 7 to 15 can enjoy getting fit, increasing flexibility, and learning important skills like anti-bullying and coordination in a supportive and fun environment. Classes are led by highly qualified, Garda-vetted instructors with over 20 years of experience. Safety and wellbeing are a top priority, and all classes follow national child protection guidelines. Classes run with the school term and pause during holidays and midterm breaks. Wear tracksuit pants and a t-shirt for your first class.
