Phonics with Robot Reg Galway

Parent-and-toddler

Join us in Oranmore and Mountbellew in County Galway! Phonics with Robot Reg Galway offers exciting, high-energy classes that introduce children aged 3 months to 5 years to early literacy skills which are the foundations for future reading success! Our award winning classes promote language development, motor skills, and phonological awareness. All classes are developed by a primary school teacher in collaboration with a speech therapist. Classes also delivered by an experience primary school teacher. Baby classes (3-12 months) focus on sensory exploration through nursery rhymes, while Minis (1-2.5 years) and Preschooler (2.5-5 years) classes build foundational skills in language and early literacy skills that are fun and engaging! Our classes run in Mountbellew ( Mondays) and Oranmore ( Tuesdays AND Saturdays) Both parents/carers are welcome to attend with their little one.
