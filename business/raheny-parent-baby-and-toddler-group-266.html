Raheny Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Raheny Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is run by volenteers. This group welcomes parents, grandparents and carers to come along with their babies and toddlers. The group has lots of toys and has a bit of a sing song. There is also tea and coffee for the adults. Note - The space is limited in the hall so the group operates on a first come, first served basis. So get there early. 📍Location: Cara Hall Community Centre Raheny, Ireland D05 WY64 ⏰Time: Every Monday 9.30 -11.00 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €4 per family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact the group through their Facebook page for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it may not align with term time.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Cara Hall Community Centre, All Saints Drive, Raheny, Dublin 5, Ireland
