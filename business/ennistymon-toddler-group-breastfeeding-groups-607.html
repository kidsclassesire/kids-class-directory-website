Ennistymon Toddler Group & Breastfeeding groups

Parent-and-toddler

Ennistymon has a number of baby and child friendly activities hosted by the North West Clare Family Resource Centre. Please see below details for toddler group, baby massage, breastfeeding support and more. 📣 Contact Details: you can contact info@northwestclarefrc.ie for any queries 📍 Location: North West Clare Family Resource Centre, Parliament Street, Ennistymon,, Co Clare, V95 NX86 Parent and Toddler Group The group is also known as "Cairde Nua" - New Friends. This group welcomes mums, dads and childminders to attend with their baby to preschooler for a fun filled morning. A great place to have a chat and meet new people while the kids play. Contact the Centre to join the group. ⏰ Day/Time: Every Friday 9.30 - 12.00 💶 Cost: €3 per family Breastfeeding Group A welcoming space for breastfeeding mums to meet up and chat with babies. Contact the Centre to join the group. ⏰ Day/Time: Every 2nd Friday 11.00 - 12.00 💶 Cost: FREE Baby Massage Suitable for babies from 6 weeks to 6 months. A great bonding experience with your baby which has many other benefits. ⏰ Day/Time: This activity runs in terms so days and times might change. speak to the centre to sign up for the next term. 💶 Cost: €50 for 5 weeks Traveller Playgroup ⏰ Day/Time: Every Tuesday 11.30 - 1.00 💶 Cost: FREE There are many other activities offered by the Centre for Older Children and Teens. Many of which are in high demand so reach out to the centre to be added to the waiting list. - Football Group - Kids Club - Homework clubs - Youth Club - Travaller Teen Girls Group - Cultural Teen Club - Teen Club
