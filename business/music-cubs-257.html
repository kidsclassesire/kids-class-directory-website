Music Cubs

Music

Parent & child music classes for babies, toddlers and big cubs aged between 0 to 5 yrs. Music Cubs classes are 30 minutes of fun, melody & play. Introduction to musical rhythms and beats & tempos. Teaching simple percussion to your bear cub. Enjoy love performances on a variety of instruments. A time to immerse yourselves in music, together. Little ones have a natural love of music. Grow that love in a gentle, fun way. Classes in various locations across Dublin

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
