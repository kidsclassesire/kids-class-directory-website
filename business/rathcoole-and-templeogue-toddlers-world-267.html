Rathcoole and Templeogue - Toddlers World

Parent-and-toddler

Toddlers World brings a fun, interactive class for babies, toddlers and preschool kids. Music, songs and movement with egg shakers, maracas, handclappers, puppets, balloons bubbles and more. Such a great way for kids to develop social skills and speech and language development. It's a lovely stepping stone to montessori. Suitable from 4 months to 4 years and beyond!!! I still have 6, 7 and 8 year olds coming back over the summer months and midterm. It's also such a lovely way connect with other mams and grandparents, for minders to bond with the child and to create life long friendships. Pre booking is essential. Spaces are limited. If you would like more information just DM me to Toddlers World Facebook group or on 0861700806. I'm also on Instagram if you'd like to see reviews of the classes. ----- Runs every Wednesday in Rathcoole Community Centre 9.30-10.45am ----- Runs every Monday, Thursday and Friday in St Judes Pastoral Centre in Templeogue, 9.45-11am.
