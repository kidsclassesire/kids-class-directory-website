Classical Kids

Developmental

Relaxed, affordable, accessible classical music concerts for parents, children, toddlers and babies in community settings. Founded by musicians Aura Stone and Sylvia Roberts, Classical Kids believe interacting in a natural way, whether that's dancing, making a noise, or playing, is key to absorbing all that the world of classical music has to offer. They have play-mats, colouring, toys and music worksheets to facilitate this musical journey. Suitable for ages 0-3 years. Regular concerts take place across Dublin - find your nearest musical feast online. And if your child falls in love with classical music, Classical Kids have their own Spotify playlist for you to enjoy in your own space.
