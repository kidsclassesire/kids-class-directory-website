Dromdaleague Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Dromdaleague Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is also known as Tiny Treasures Playgroup. All parents and care givers are welcomed to pop in and join the group. Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
