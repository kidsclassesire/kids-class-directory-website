Sligo Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Sligo Parent and Toddler Group Co Sligo is hosted by Sligo Family Resource Centre. The resource center aims to provide facilitators for these sessions and provide a programme of music and drama/messy play to develop toddlers’ language and movement. Followed by tea/coffee and healthy snacks for the toddlers. Associated with the drama group is there is an annual Christmas party with Santa and his helpers held in the FRC every December and a Summer Picnic to Doorly Park in June. 📍 Location: Sligo Family Resource Centre, 49 The Mall, Sligo ⏰Day/Time: Every Tuesday & Wednesday from 10.30 -11.30 (during school term) 💶 Cost: FREE per Family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 071 91 46315 for any queries **Dads Group The Family Resource Centre also hosts a regular Dad's Group where fathers can come together and share their experiences of fatherhood. Check with the centre or their social media to find out when the next meeting will take part.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: 071 91 46315
Address: 49 The Mall, Rathquarter, Sligo, Ireland
