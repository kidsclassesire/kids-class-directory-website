The Toy Box - Sensory Family Fun

Parent-and-toddler

The Toy Box runs a sensory friendly playgroup with the addition of a toy library too!! They also run events throughout the year. So keep an eye on social media for upcoming events and playgroup dates. Join us for The Toy Box Family Fun Day! A fun, inclusive event for children of all ages – especially suited for ages 6 months to 8 years. Enjoy a Zumbini class, sensory play, face painting, the amazing SENCO sensory van, and surprise character visits! Taking place at Fortlawn Community Centre on Saturday, 7th June from 12–2pm. Tickets are €15 per child with sibling discounts available. A relaxed, neurodiverse-friendly environment for families to connect, play, and explore. Book now on Eventbrite – all ages welcome!
