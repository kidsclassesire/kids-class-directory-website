Lullababy Baby Classes

Developmental

Baby Massage classes and Baby Reflexology classes are for babies from birth to 12 months. Classes last 90 minutes and usually start at 10am, running in terms - Baby Massage is 6 weeks, while Baby Reflexology is 5 weeks. Baby Yoga is suitable for babies from 12 weeks to pre-crawling, and the term runs for 4 weeks. Location: An Seomra, Rm 23, Gorey Business Park.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
