Lullababy Baby Classes

Developmental

Baby Massage classes and Baby Reflexology classes are for babies from birth to 12 months. The classes last 90 mins and usually start at 10am. The classes run in terms Baby Massage Classes are 6 weeks, while Baby Reflexology is 5 weeks long. Baby Yoga is suitable for babies from 12 weeks to pre-crawling and the term runs for 4 weeks. 📍 Location: An Seomra, Rm 23, Gorey Business Park.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
