Art Classes/Private mentoring

Arts and crafts

We offer private art mentoring, 1-on-1 or in groups, on a regular basis or as a once-off - perfect if you're looking for a rainy day activity when visiting Westport. We have classes for children and adults throughout the year, available in blocks of 4-8 weeks, with options to drop in occasionally (more so throughout the summer). Whether you're a local wanting a hand with a project, want to master a certain skill, or are visiting with your family and want to try something different and spend a couple of hours watercolour painting on a rainy day, contact us to organise the perfect activity for you. We cover watercolour, acrylic painting, drawing, basic crafts and card making.
