The Unrelated Auntie- Messy Play Group (facilitated by a play therapist)

Developmental

Hourly slot of messy play- different age groups. Based in North Kerry. Great opportunity for parents/carers to have some 1:1 playtime with their little ones. This is not a drop off class and parents/guardians are required to stay- tea/coffee available. Great social opportunity for both children and for parents/carers to meet and chat to other adults.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
