The Unrelated Auntie- Messy Play Group (facilitated by a play therapist)

Developmental

An hourly slot of messy play for different age groups, based in North Kerry - a great opportunity for parents/carers to have some 1:1 playtime with their little ones. This is not a drop-off class, and parents/guardians are required to stay; tea/coffee available. A great social opportunity for both children and for parents/carers to meet and chat with other adults.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: TheUnrelatedAuntie@hotmail.com
Address: Kerry, County Kerry, Ireland
