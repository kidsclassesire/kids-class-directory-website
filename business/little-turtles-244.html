Little Turtles

Parent-and-toddler

We are Leah & Molly, owners of Little Turtles Playgroup! We are based in Funky Monkeys, Spraoi and Jungle Den Playcentres. All these classes are pay as you go and no need to pre-book - all prices for these sessions vary between centres. We run seasonal events around Halloween, Christmas and Easter. Where we have a fun morning of fun activities and games based on the season we are celebrating. We also run outdoor summer classes which we are SO excited to launch again. Below you can find out more details or follow us on Instagram ‘littleturtles___’ Little Turtles is a vibrant and engaging class designed for our youngest learners, aimed at children aged 0-6 years. Our meticulously planned activities are tailored to meet the developmental needs of the children, ensuring that they are constantly learning while they play and have fun. Our priority is to create an environment where play is not just an activity, but an integral part of learning and growth. We are focused on fostering a caring, thoughtful, and friendly atmosphere where children can laugh, have fun, and thrive while learning.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
