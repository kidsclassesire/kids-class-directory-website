Thurles / Roscrea / Cashel /Tipperary Town Toddler Group Story Time

Parent-and-toddler

Libraries across Tipperary have great classes on offer for babies, toddlers and kids. Toddler Storytime is a great toddler group for a quieter and calmer atmosphere. The libraries have some toys and the staff read lovely books and often do nursery rhymes too.
