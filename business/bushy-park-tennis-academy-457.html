Bushy Park Tennis Academy

Tennis

Bushy Park Tennis Academy offers fun and engaging tennis lessons for children aged 5 to 17. Classes are organised by age and skill level to ensure an enjoyable and supportive experience for all players. Lesson times: Monday to Thursday, 3-6pm; Saturday, 9am-1pm. Lessons take place at Bushy Park Tennis and Padel Club, providing a great environment for learning and playing.

Contact: 01 490 5960
Address: Rathdown Ave, Terenure, Dublin 6W, Co. Dublin, D6W TX24
