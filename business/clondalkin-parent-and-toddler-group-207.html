Clondalkin Parent and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Clondalkin/Knockmitten Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Dublin Clondalkin Parent and Toddler Group also known as 2S group is for preschool aged kids to have free time to play, enjoy messy/sensory play and do some arts and crafts. Parents can mingle and chat to feel less alone in parenting. Location: Knockmitten community centre, Monksfield Lawns, Clondalkin, Dublin Day/Time: Every Monday at 10am -11.30pm (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €2.50 per family

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Address: Monksfield Lawns, Clondalkin, Dublin 22, Ireland
