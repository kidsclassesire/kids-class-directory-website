Summer Surf Camps

Team Sports

We offer a fun and thrill packed pack week of surfing. Each day’s two hour session will allow your children to gain confidence and skills in the water. Our Camps are more than just surf lessons. On the first day kids have a complete lesson on the beach and then into the water to catch their first waves. Over the rest of the week they will learn more about ocean safety and surfing. We will also work with them to catch lots of waves in the water and have loads of fun. And to finish out a great week we hold an award ceremony at the end. This recognises your child’s hard work and celebrates them becoming a surfer. We group participants in order of age and experience. On arrival for your surf camp in Enniscrone we will meet you and introduce you to your lovely surf instructors. They will be working with your child for the week. After completing a registration form, we will then choose a wetsuit and surfboard that suits your child best. We have winter and summer wetsuits which allows them to stay toasty warm during an Irish summer! On very cool days we can add hoods for extra warmth. Once you have your child’s wetsuit, we will explain how to put the wetsuit on. We will make sure that they are comfortable as this is the wetsuit they will have for the week. When everyone is dressed and ready the instructors will introduce themselves. Everyone gets a board and heads for the beach and the fun begins. Emphasis in our camps is on fun and safety. We pride ourselves on our child-friendly camps. We provide full supervision on land and in water. We have been running our surf camps in Enniscrone for the last 18 years and kids return to us year after year. If you would like to hear what they have to say check out our reviews on Trip Advisor.
