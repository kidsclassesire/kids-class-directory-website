Castledermot Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Kildare Castledermot Parent, Baby and Toddler Group also known as Tir na Nog. All welcome never to young to join in. Come and enjoy meeting new people and let the little ones play. Enjoy a cop of tea and buscuits while the kiddies make new friends. 📌 No booking needed, just pop in. Location: Teach Community Center, Castledermot Community Center, Castledermot, Ireland Day/Time: Every Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9.30am -12pm (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €2 per family

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Castledermot, County Kildare, Ireland
