Walkinstown/Kimmage/Crumlin Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Crumlin Parent, Baby and Toddler Group. Why not come along for a cuppa, a chat and the chance to share your parenting experiences. Give your toddlers a morning out interacting with other children of a similar age while experiencing activities such as art time, song time, free play and snack time. The group is babies up to 3 years.
