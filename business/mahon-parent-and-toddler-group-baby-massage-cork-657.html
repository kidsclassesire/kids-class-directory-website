Mahon Parent and Toddler Group & Baby Massage Cork

Parent-and-toddler

Mahon Parent and Toddler Group is run by the Mahon Family Resource Centre. Enjoy a coffee and a chat with other parents, fun and activities with your child. Location: Mahon Dr, Mahon, Cork, T12 Y180 Day/Time: Every Friday during School Terms at 10am -11.30. Cost: €6 per family The Centre at Times also hosts Baby Massage Classes at a discounted rate, so check out the groups Facebook page.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Mahon, Cork, T12 Y180, Ireland
