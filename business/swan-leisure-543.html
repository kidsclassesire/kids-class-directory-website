Swan Leisure

Swimming

Swan Leisure in Rathmines offers high-quality children’s swimming lessons in a welcoming, family-friendly environment. With a strong focus on water safety, skill development, and fun, their structured programme supports swimmers of all levels—from beginners to more advanced. Led by experienced instructors, Swan Leisure’s lessons take place in a modern, well-maintained pool with a warm and supportive atmosphere that encourages confidence and progression. Conveniently located and loved by local families, it’s an ideal spot for children to learn essential swimming skills while enjoying every splash along the way.
