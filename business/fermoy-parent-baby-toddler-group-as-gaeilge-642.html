Fermoy Parent, Baby & Toddler Group "As Gaeilge"

Parent-and-toddler

Fermoy Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is also known as the Dancing Daisies Playgroup. This is an Irish Speaking playgroup. Everyone is welcome, a good way to start learning Irish by picking up a couple of words. The event will start with a few rhymes, then a few games and will finish with toys and a cup of tea and coffee available for parents. This event is aimed at all levels of Irish and is open to all. This group is a great opportunity for parents to come together and talk to each other. 📍Location: Anderson Room, Fermoy Youth Centre, Ashe Quay, Fermoy 🗓️⏰Time: Every 2nd Friday from 10.00 - 11.30 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €FREE per family 📣 Contact Details: you can contact the facebook page for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: 086 1655053
