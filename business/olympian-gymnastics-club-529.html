Olympian Gymnastics Club

Gymnastics

Olympian Gymnastics is Ireland’s largest gymnastics provider, offering over 180 classes per week across eight venues. They focus on non-competitive, movement-based gymnastics that promotes social development and interaction for kids aged 18 months to 16 years. | With a welcoming environment and a wide range of classes, Olympian Gymnastics helps children build confidence, coordination, and fitness through fun and engaging activities.
