Butterfly Bambino’s - Baby Massage

Developmental

Parents/caregivers get the opportunity to bond with their baby, learn massage strokes/tummy time positions, and have a cuppa while chatting with other caregivers/parents. These baby massage classes are suitable from birth to pre-movers. Classes are approximately 1.5 hours long and currently run on a Thursday morning at the Ballyneety Hub, Limerick.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
Contact: butterflybambinos@outlook.ie
Address: Glen, County Limerick, V94 7W13, Ireland
