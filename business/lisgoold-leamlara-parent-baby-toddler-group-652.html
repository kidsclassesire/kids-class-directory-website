Lisgoold/Leamlara Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Lisgoold/Leamlara Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is open to mums, dads and all carers. Come along for Toys, games, crafts, dress up, ride on toys. They even have a baby corner and snacks for the kids.
