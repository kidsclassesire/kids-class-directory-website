Dundrum South Dublin Athletic Club (DSD)

Athletics Drop-off

Ireland's largest athletics club, offering juvenile athletics training covering track & field, cross country and road running on its own 400m Mondo track and trail campus in the Dublin mountains foothills.

Verified 2026-07-18
Schedule: Ongoing membership
Ages: Juvenile (approx. primary to secondary school age)
Supervision: Drop-off
Membership fees and training group times are managed through the Athletics Ireland registration portal and were not published as static pricing. As of late 2025, the club has a significant waiting list for new juvenile members due to high demand.
