Kinvara Parent Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Kinvarra Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Galway. This is a great place to meet new friends and create a community. The groups welcomes mums, dads and carers to po along with the tots for a fun morning. There are plenty of toys to play with and a great way for the kids to socialise. The group often has special guests attending to run fun workshops and they host an annual jumble sale (great way to grab a bargain) 📍Location: Kinvara Community Centre, Glebe Road Kinvara, Ireland H91 WC64 🗓️⏰Time: Every Wednesday 10.00 - 12.00 (during school term) 💶 Cost: Donations usually €2-€4 🧸Contact Details: you can contact karenconneely@gmail.com for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
