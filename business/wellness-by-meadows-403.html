Wellness by Meadows

Developmental

Our Baby Massage Classes run for a 5 week term usually, Wednesday mornings 10-11.30am here at "Wellness by Meadows", just outside Tinahely, Co Wicklow. Y14VY70 Baby Massage provides numerous physical, psychological and emotional benefits for babies and their families. Each week you will learn different massage strokes that can help: ❤️Relax and soothe your baby ❤️Aid digestion to relieve wind, colic and constipation ❤️Help baby sleep well ❤️Teach your baby body awareness. ❤️Your baby will also enjoy the songs and rhymes throughout the class. Connecting with other Parents in these classes is also another big plus, with time for a cuppa and chat at the end of each class. Class sizes are kept small. We can also accommodate for 1:1 classes, both parents attending class, grandparents may also like to attend. We also cater for babies that may have special needs. Handouts and oil are provided for the class. I recommend wearing comfy clothes for you and baby. Just bring along what ever you usually bring with your baby and a warm blanket to wrap baby in. Classes are baby led with lots of time for feeding, changing and some nod off to sleep. Course investment is €120 for the 5 weeks. (You can also claim back most of the cost through your health insurance policy depending on what policy you may have)

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
