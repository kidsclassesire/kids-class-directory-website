Encore! School of Performing Arts

Music

Encore Tots is a drama and singing class for children age 3 to 5 years! We have a programme of poems, songs, rhymes, movement to music and fun and games that are designed to build confidence and performing arts skills. Parents do not attend the class, which is 30 minutes long. We provide a free t-shirt and students just wear anything comfy to facilitate free movement. Our staff are extremely experienced and kind, children love them!

Ages: 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
