Pembroke

Hockey

Pembroke Juniors provides a safe, respectful, and supportive environment for young players to develop both individual and team hockey skills. Welcoming boys and girls from Junior Infants to 4th Year (boys) and 6th Year (girls), the focus is on learning, fun, and personal growth through sport. Coaches promote teamwork, sportsmanship, and respect, while offering high-quality, enjoyable training for all levels. The regular season runs from September to April, with plenty of weekend hockey, and summer activities including youth tournaments and social mixed-gender games.
