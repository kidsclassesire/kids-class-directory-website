Swim Max - Baby & Parent Group Swimming Lessons in Limerick

Swimming

Baby & Parent Group Swimming Lessons at Swim Max, for babies aged 6 months to 4.5 years, offer much more than just an introduction to water - we teach life-saving skills through water safety, hydrotherapy, and aqua gymnastics, all designed to support your child's developmental growth. In our interactive lessons, you and your baby will enjoy bonding while having fun. Parents don't need to know how to swim, as lessons are held in a shallow pool and either mum or dad can take part. 30-minute lessons. Why choose us? A unique teaching methodology developed by an experienced instructor and mum, and small class sizes for focused attention. Available on weekdays and weekends. Location: Swim Max, Unit 5A Delta Retail Park, Ballysimon Road, Limerick, V94 YW58. Call 061 545 454, or book directly at https://www.swimmax.ie/timetables/babies/babytoddler-parent-swimming-lessons
