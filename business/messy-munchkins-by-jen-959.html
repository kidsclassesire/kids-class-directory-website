Messy Munchkins by Jen

Arts and crafts

Join us for fun, hands-on messy play every Tuesday, 10:30-11:30am, at Cheekpoint Community Hall, Crossroad, Cheekpoint, Co. Waterford. These classes are perfect for toddlers, with activities designed to stimulate their senses and creativity. Caregivers of all kinds are welcome - mums, dads, grandparents, and childminders - and are required to stay for the duration of the class, making it a great opportunity to bond and enjoy the experience together. Please dress your child in clothes that can get messy, and bring a change of clothes just in case; all other materials are provided. We also offer weekend sessions for ages 3-6 years. Contact me for more information - come join the fun.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old
