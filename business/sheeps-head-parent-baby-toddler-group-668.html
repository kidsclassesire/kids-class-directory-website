Sheeps Head Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Durrus Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork. Is a lovely community playgroup. Pop along with your little one for a chat other other families. Refreshments are on offer too. The group hosts events for Christmas and have a really lovely swap shop event to pass on items your little on has outgrown. 📍Location: Durrus Community Hall 🗓️⏰Time: Every Wednesday from 10.00 - 12.00 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €3 per child or a family for €4 📣 Contact Details: you can contact the group through their social media accounts for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Durrus, County Cork, Ireland
