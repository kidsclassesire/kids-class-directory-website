Sound and Scribble

Parent-and-toddler

Sound and Scribble is a playgroup that has stations based on sound, scibble, structure, sugradh and sensory. Mothers, father's, grandparents and their littles are all welcome. Supervision is required for duration of the playgroup. Runs on Fridays 9.30am -10.30am in Scouts Den, Castlebar. A change of clothes might be needed as often we have a messy play station.

Ages: 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old
