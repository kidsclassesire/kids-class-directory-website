Bloom Baby Classes North East Ireland

Developmental

We have two classes on offer. Caterpillar Club for babies aged 0-6 months and Busy Bees for babies aged 6-15 months. 🐛 Caterpillar Club (Birth - 6 Months) 🐛 A gentle and calming introduction to our musical sensory classes. These sessions are designed to support all areas of baby development, with a strong focus on parent and baby bonding. Our Caterpillar Club classes awaken baby’s early senses and provide stimulation in a fun yet soothing environment using music, sensory props, lights, and movement. We finish each class with our magical chill parachute and a short guided baby massage to encourage relaxation and hopefully a lovely long nap afterwards! 🐝 Busy Bees (6-15 Months) 🐝 A fun, lively musical development class designed to help babies grow in confidence as they explore the world around them. Activities encourage fine and gross motor skills, body awareness, early language development, rhythm, and social interaction. Building on the gentle foundations of Caterpillar Club, Busy Bees introduces instruments, movement, and our amazing branded props to support development in all areas through play and music. ✨ Different Theme Every Week! ✨ Our classes feature a brand new theme every week for a whole year! During theme time, babies can enjoy themed music, dress-up, sensory lights, fun props, and heuristic treasure baskets. Busy Bees love interacting with each other while exploring the themed activities, while our Caterpillar babies can enjoy tummy time alongside sensory lights and themed props. Theme time is also a lovely opportunity for parents to relax, chat, and build friendships within our Bloom community.
