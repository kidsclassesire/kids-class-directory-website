Bloom Baby Classes North East Ireland

Developmental

Two classes are on offer: Caterpillar Club for babies aged 0-6 months, and Busy Bees for babies aged 6-15 months. Caterpillar Club is a gentle and calming introduction to our musical sensory classes, designed to support all areas of baby development with a strong focus on parent and baby bonding. Classes awaken baby's early senses and provide stimulation in a fun yet soothing environment using music, sensory props, lights, and movement, finishing with a chill parachute and a short guided baby massage to encourage relaxation and, hopefully, a long nap afterwards. Busy Bees is a fun, lively musical development class designed to help babies grow in confidence as they explore the world around them, encouraging fine and gross motor skills, body awareness, early language development, rhythm, and social interaction. Building on the gentle foundations of Caterpillar Club, Busy Bees introduces instruments, movement, and branded props to support development in all areas through play and music. Classes feature a new theme every week for a whole year: during theme time, babies enjoy themed music, dress-up, sensory lights, fun props, and heuristic treasure baskets. Busy Bees love interacting with each other while exploring the themed activities, while Caterpillar babies can enjoy tummy time alongside sensory lights and themed props. Theme time is also an opportunity for parents to relax, chat, and build friendships within the Bloom community.
