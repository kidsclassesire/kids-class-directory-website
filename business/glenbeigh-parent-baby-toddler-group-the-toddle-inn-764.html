Glenbeigh Parent, Baby & Toddler Group (The Toddle Inn)

Parent-and-toddler

Glenbeigh Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Kerry is also known as "The Toddle Inn" playgroup. It is a great opportunity to meet other parents in the area and have a cup of tea and a chat. The kids really enjoy meeting other kids and there is a great choice of new toys and books to suit all pre-school ages 📍Location: Tigh Beithe Community Centre, Glenbeigh, V93 N5TE ( (next to the church) 🗓️⏰Time: Every Thursday 9.30 - 11.00 (during school term) 💶 Cost: €4 per Family 📣 Contact Details: you can contact the facebook page for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Kilnabrack Upper, County Kerry, V93 N5TE, Ireland
