Clonmel / Cahir Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Clonmel / Cahir Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Tipperary is hosted by Clonmel Community Mother Programme. Parents no longer need to feel isolated, children have the opportunity to play in a safe, happy environment. Mother & Baby/Parent & Toddler groups form the stepping stones to pre-school/primary school. Everyone is welcome, it is a good place to meet other mothers and let the babies and toddlers play.
