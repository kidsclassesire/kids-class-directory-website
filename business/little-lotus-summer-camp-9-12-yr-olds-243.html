Little Lotus Summer camp 9-12 yr olds

Introducing our Little Lotus Summer Sessions - the perfect way for kids aged 9-12 to unplug, connect, and have a total blast. July 8th: Canvas Painting & Snack Board - paint a sunset scene and enjoy a custom cheese & fruit snack board. July 9th: Reset & Recharge DIY Spa - hand-craft your own fizzy bath bomb and make and apply a luxurious face mask. July 10th: The Sensory Scientist - create vibrant, customised slime to bring home and take on the Sensory Mystery Box Challenge. Location: Belmayne, Dublin 13. Time: 10am-12pm each day.
