Babble & Bloom

Developmental

Parent and baby early communication courses Learn evidence-based strategies to support your baby's communication from the very beginning. Created and led by a registered Speech & Language Therapist. You’ll learn how to: Model baby signs Build connection and language through play Turn everyday moments into communication opportunities All through relaxed, play-based sessions. Suitable for babies and toddlers aged 6–14 months, Babble & Bloom offers a relaxed and supportive environment to connect with your baby, build confidence in supporting their development, and meet other parents at a similar stage. Join us for six weeks of play and practical learning that will help you nurture your baby’s communication skills in everyday moments. Please note: While Babble & Bloom is created and led by a Speech & Language Therapist, it is an educational class rather than a Speech & Language Therapy service. Participation does not constitute assessment or therapy, and the course is not a substitute for speech and language assessment or intervention where needed.
