Messy Adventures

Developmental

Play promotes physical development, encouraging children to practise their fine and gross motor skills as well as hand-eye coordination. Children are given the opportunity to explore their senses by using their whole bodies and developing these movements. Our classes allow parents and carers to play one-on-one with their children, and also encourage children to learn how to engage in activities independently as well as with other children - all vital skills for their development. We pride ourselves on providing classes which are fully inclusive, warmly welcoming all babies and children. Tuesday: Malahide, 11am (messy play).
