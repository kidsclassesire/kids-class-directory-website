Castletown Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Castletown Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is also known as CAS TODDLERS. This is a popular group as it runs on a Saturday. Lots of toys for the kids and a great place for them to interact with other children of a similar age. The group holds events for special holidays like Halloween and often have special activities like baking throughout the year. Stay up to date on the activities through their Facebook page. 📍Location: Castletown Hall , Castletown Kinneigh, Ireland 🗓️⏰Time: Every Saturday 10.30 - 12.00 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: €5 per family 📣 Contact Details: you can contact castletownparentsandtoddlers@gmail.com for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: castletownparentsandtoddlers@gmail.com
