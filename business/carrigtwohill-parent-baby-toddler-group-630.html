Carrigtwohill Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Carrigtwohill Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is hosted by Carrigtwohill Family Resource Centre. The Parent and Toddler Group play’s a significant role in promoting community development and integration. It is a local support group for parents and carers and encourages friendships, bonding and learning for both children and adults. Children of all ages are welcome, from babes in arms to 2-3 years old, typical activities include: Free play, play dough, arts and crafts. The group enhances opportunities to develop a support network and involvement in other activities and groups. The group operates as a pay as you go class so drop in at a time that suits.
