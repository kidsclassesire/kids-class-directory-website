Dlr Leisure

Sports

dlr Leisure provides a wide range of sports, from indoor and outdoor sports to water sports. All sports activities are delivered by quality coaches who make participation fun for everyone, no matter their current level of ability. All coaches are NGB qualified and Garda screened. Small groups at all sessions ensure maximum attention for all participants. dlr Leisure's Sports Section provides all sports equipment at the sessions, but it's important for parents/guardians to ensure their child is dressed appropriately to take part in the activity and to bring a non-fizzy drink with them. dlr Leisure also runs various kids' camps throughout the school holidays.
