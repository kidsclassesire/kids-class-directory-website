Colour and Art Kids classes Balbriggan

Arts and crafts

Colour and Art Kids caters for children aged between 5-12 years. All aspects of creativity are supported, and each child's individual interests are developed through expressive art. Held at Castlelands Community Centre: Wednesday (5-7 years), 4.30-5.30pm; Wednesday (8-12 years), 5.30-6.30pm. New term starting in September.

Ages: 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old, 12 year old
Contact: 0868877467 · info@colourandart.ie
Address: Castlelands, Balbriggan, County Dublin, Ireland
