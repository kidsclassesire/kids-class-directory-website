Railway Union Hockey Club

Hockey

Railway Union Hockey Club has a long-standing tradition of family and community values, earning its reputation as “the family club” in Irish hockey circles. | ​ | The junior section is open to boys aged 5+ and girls from 1st class (age 7+), offering a supportive and fun environment to learn and enjoy hockey. | ​ | With a strong focus on playing the game the right way, the club fosters respect, teamwork, and a lifelong love of the sport.
