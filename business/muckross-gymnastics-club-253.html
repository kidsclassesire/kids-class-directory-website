Muckross Gymnastics Club

Gymnastics

MPGC provides a range of gymnastics classes from Preschool Gymnastics (3+) through to Teen classes (12+) and open play for babies to preschoolers. The majority of our gymnasts take part in our one hour per week, Recreational Classes, which take place all week in our venue in Sandyford Business Park in Dublin 18 👉ARTISTIC GYMNASTICS CLASSES: Our gymnastics classes are focused on fun, learning new skills as well as gaining strength, flexibility and discipline that helps our gymnasts in all aspects of their lives. We train on Asymmetric Bars, Balance Beam, Vault and Floor. Along with learning new skills on the trampoline and AirTrack. ➕EXTRA REC: Perfect for any of our Recreational gymnasts (1 hour & 1.5 per week groups) who would like to take a second class to build on their skills. 🥷NINJA CLASS: For boys and girls who love to run and jump and climb. A parkour style class that will keep you moving with lots of obstacle courses and fun challenges. 🦘TRAMPLINE CLASS: For girls and boys who love to jump! Trampoline classes will use our Olympic size trampoline, AirTrack, mini tramp and so much more. 👶TOTS OPEN PLAY: Want somewhere to let your little one run and play? Our Tots Open Play is open to crawlers to age 4 (non school girls and boys). 👫Parent supervised play in our gym, with lots of play to run and bounce and swing and climb. Tots Sessions aren't coach-led but we are on hand to help and play. Drop in anytime, and stay for the whole session or just a little bit. 👉Sibling discounts available. 💳Don't forget to ask for our loyalty card
