Mucky Picnic

Developmental

The most regular Mucky Picnic is on Friday mornings in Kidspace Rathcoole. These run during school terms at 10:15am. No need to book, just turn up ready for mess! Children must not be left unattended. Each class has a selection of sensory and messy play trays to explore, which vary from week to week depending on the theme. Sessions are relaxed and unstructured, so you and your little one can explore at your own pace - a relaxing environment for little ones to play, socialise and be their own unique selves. Just like a real picnic, Mucky Picnic sessions can be taken almost anywhere - sessions also run in libraries, schools, toddler groups, community centres and play centres. Follow us on social media to stay up to date on the latest terms and classes. Let's go on an adventure to make, mess and play together.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
