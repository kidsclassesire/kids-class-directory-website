Fireflies Leixlip Parent and Kids Group

Parent-and-toddler

Fireflies Leixlip Parent and Kids Group Fireflies is a social club for children with autism and complex needs. The age group is for 3-7years, It is a free play group with different sensory stations for the children to explore as they wish. The hall is large and secure and offer a safe space for children and parents to meet each week. "Let's Play the sensory way!" Location: Leixlip Youth & Community Centre, Leixlip, Co. Kildare Day/Time: Every Wednesday from 5.30pm -7pm (during term time)

Ages: 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old
Address: Newtown, Leixlip, County Kildare, W23 T8W5, Ireland
