Kids Yoga & Zumbini Combo Class Midleton

Developmental

Preschool Yoga & Zumbini Combo Class takes place in My Place Midleton on Thursdays from 3.30-4.15pm. The Zumbini portion of the class includes singing, dancing and playing instruments, introducing children to music from various cultural backgrounds with a child-centred, developmentally appropriate approach. The yoga includes age-appropriate themed yoga stories, such as jungle adventures, farm adventures, missions to the moon, and ocean dives, exploring common issues kids experience such as managing frustration, friendships, and separation anxiety through the characters they meet. The stories include visuals and props to aid understanding. The class also includes mindfulness activities, easy-to-follow games, dance, and a little relaxation to finish. The secret ingredient is playfulness, keeping your child motivated, excited and moving, while the classes also work on children's emotional intelligence, provide coping tools, and enhance social skills.
