Tralee (St Brendan's) Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Tralee Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Kerry runs during term time. It is an inviting space for mums, dads, grandparents and all caregivers to come and meet new people. Kids will have a great time with all the toys on offer. Coffee/tea is available too. Keep an eye on the groups socials as they sometimes run a buggy walking group during the summer. 📍Location: St Brendan's Pastoral Centre Tralee, Ireland V92 XA36 🗓️⏰Time: Every Monday 10.00- 11.30 (during school term) 💶 Cost: €2.50 per family 📣 Contact Details: you can contact the group through their socials for queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old
Address: Rahoonane, Tralee, County Kerry, V92 XA36, Ireland
