Irishtown Parent and Toddler Group -Quarryvale Family Resource Centre

Parent-and-toddler

Clondalkin Parent, Baby and Toddler Activities Quarryvale Family Resource Centre hosts many great groups and events for young families. ** Little Rascal's Baby and Toddler Group Come and join us at our Baby & Toddler group where parents can chat and socialise, and where the children have a chance to play and develop new skills and learn to interact with others. No need to register in advance. Time: Every Wednesday from 10 a.m. to 11.30 a.m. during school term time Cost: €3/family, all materials and snacks included. ** Mother and Baby Yoga Mother & Baby Yoga allows you to bond with your baby while exercising safely, helping to strengthen your mind and body after giving birth. ** Infant Massage Infant massage promotes bonding and positive attachment. It support the physical and mental well-being of both the baby and parent. Keep an eye on their socials to sign up for these classes. Highly recommended Location: Quarryvale Family Resource Centre, Shancastle Avenue, Clondalkin, Dublin 22 / Greenfort Gardens, Clondalkin, Dublin 22, D22 V6T0

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
