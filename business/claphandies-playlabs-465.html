ClapHandies PlayLabs

Developmental

If you're seeking joyous, playful classes for your little one, ClapHandies PlayLabs deliver in spades. Designed for babies, wobblers, and toddlers (birth to around 3.5 years), every session blends music, movement, sensory play, rhyme, and storytelling, creating moments of connection and discovery. At the helm is Liza Crotty, the dreamer and doer behind ClapHandies. With more than 15 years of guiding Dublin families into playful learning, Liza infuses every class with heart, creativity, and infectious energy. We adore ClapHandies because, under Liza's vision, play becomes poetry, parent-child bonds deepen, and early years are filled with wonder, laughter, and very happy toddlers.
