Carrick on Shannon Parent and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Carrick on Shannon Parent and Toddler Group is hosted by Breffni Family Resource Centre. Parents, grandparents, carers, are all welcome to bring their baby or toddler to this playgroup. Toys, Games and activities for your little ones while parents and carers have a nice cuppa and get to know each other. The group also schedules lots of activities from, dancing, music, playing and messy play. 📍 Location: The Cabin, Breffni Family Resource Centre, Breffni Crescent, Carrick-on-Shannon, Co.
