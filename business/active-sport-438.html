Active Sport

Sports

Active Sport Ireland has been promoting fun, healthy, and inclusive sports opportunities for kids since 2005. Founded by Mark Whitwood, the group now offers a range of activity programmes to keep young people active and engaged: Active Sport, multi-sport activities for children aged 4-11; Active Totz, play-based physical activities for toddlers aged 18 months to 4 years; and Active Soccer, football camps and classes for kids aged 4-11. With professional coaching and a commitment to quality, Active Sport Ireland helps combat inactivity and encourages kids to develop a love for sports.
