Baby Health

Developmental

Baby massage classes taught by an internationally certified instructor. All parents/caregivers are welcome. Each session is 1 hour. Classes in person in Dublin or online, available in English, Spanish or Italian. Baby massage is more than just a soothing practice, it is a powerful tool that nurtures a child's emotional and physical development while strengthening the bond between you as a parent and your little one. It supports relaxation and brain development, soothes teething pain, encourages better sleep, eases digestion and colic, boosts muscle and joint strength, and improves flexibility, circulation and overall physical health.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
