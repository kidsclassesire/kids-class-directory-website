Arabian Gymnastics Club

Gymnastics

Arabian Gymnastics Club offers recreational and competitive gymnastics classes for boys and girls of all ages. Located in a 10,000 sq ft facility in Baldoyle, Dublin, the club is equipped with a range of gymnastics equipment, including sprung floors, airtracks, bars, beams, vaults, trampolines, and resi mats. | Established in 2005 as Dublin’s first full-time gymnastics facility, Arabian Gymnastics provides structured training in artistic gymnastics, focusing on skill progression and fitness development. The club also runs holiday camps throughout the year to give children additional opportunities to practice and improve.
