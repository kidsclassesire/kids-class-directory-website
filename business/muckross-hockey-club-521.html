Muckross Hockey Club

Hockey

Muckross Hockey Club’s thriving Junior Section welcomes girls from 1st class to 4th year, offering a fun, inclusive introduction to hockey built on friendship, teamwork, and community spirit. | ​ | Sessions run weekly, with Sunday morning Academy Hockey for younger age groups and Friday evening training for older players. | Many juniors go on to join the club’s senior squads, staying involved through their teens, college years, and beyond. | ​ | Junior membership applications open each May for the season beginning in September. If applications exceed available spots, places are allocated by lottery.

Address: Marlborough Rd, Donnybrook, Dublin 4, Ireland
