Blanchardstown Parent and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Blanchardstown Parent and Toddler Group is brought to you by Blanchardstown Centre. Offering an amazing FREE Sillybilly Music & Story Classes for Babies and Toddlers. The Sillybilly Music and Story Classes introduce a love of Music, Songs & Rhythm, Instruments, Stories and Imagination to your Child. The Sillybilly Music & Story Program educates Children giving them the confidence for life. If you haven’t been before come along with your little one for some great fun, music, dancing and singing, you will love it! 📍Location: Outside the Odeon, Blanchardstown Centre Blanchardstown Rd S, Blanchardstown, Dublin ⏰Time: Every 10am and 11am every Wednesday (during term time) 💶 Cost: FREE

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Address: Blanchardstown Centre, Blanchardstown, Dublin 15, Ireland
