Do Your Dance Tots

Dance

☀️ Do Your Dance Tots: Saturday, 10:15 - 11:00 (ages 2 - 4) - Newtownpark Pastoral Centre, Blackrock 🌟 Do Your Dance Tots – Where Little Feet Find Big Fun! 🌟 Perfect for tiny dancers around 2 years old (but younger kiddos are welcome if they're ready to groove)! 🎶 Watch your little ones light up as they build strength, balance, and coordination through playful, follow-along dances, interactive games, and joyful singing throughout the 45 minute class. 🎉 Every class is a mini-adventure filled with movement and music, helping your tots develop key motor skills while having a blast! 💃🕺 All children are welcome - Do Your Dance is a completely inclusive environment. If your child has additional needs, please let us know before class so we can ensure they have the best experience possible. Join us for giggles, wiggles, and endless fun - because at Do Your Dance Tots, it’s all about moving, learning, and laughing together! 🥳 Parking: Parking is plentiful and free, so you’ll have no trouble finding a spot! During the Class: Room 2 isn’t the biggest, so we encourage parents to wait just outside in the comfy couch area. The door will remain open, so feel free to peek in, take photos, or even join your child in the fun if you’d like! If you'd prefer to stay with your child the entire time, just let us know—we’re happy to accommodate! Some tots love dancing on their own, and that’s perfectly okay too! Parents must stay on the premises for the duration of the class. What to Bring: Please dress your child in comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. Make sure your little one brings a water bottle/drink with them. There’s also a water fountain in the centre and bathrooms available for use. Arrival Time: Please arrive 10 minutes before class to get settled and ready to dance. Can't make it? No worries! If something comes up, just let us know via phone (0877007298) or email (doyourdancefitness@gmail.com) 24 hours before class. We can’t wait to see your little one dance, play, and have a blast with us! 🌟

Ages: 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
