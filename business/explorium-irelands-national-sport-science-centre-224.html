Explorium Irelands National Sport & Science Centre

Developmental

"A fun, hands-on playdough session where toddlers can squish, roll, and shape their own creations! This activity encourages creativity, fine motor skills, and sensory play, while also giving little ones the chance to socialise, share ideas, and play together." Mondays and Thursdays Playdough Session runs from 10am - 10.30am but toddlers ticket include 2 hours exploration of Junior Explorium All Mums, Dads, Grandparents and caregivers welcome to stay Free Car Parking Coffee Shop onsite

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Contact: junior@explorium.ie
Address: Sandyford, Dublin, D18 N1F2, Ireland
