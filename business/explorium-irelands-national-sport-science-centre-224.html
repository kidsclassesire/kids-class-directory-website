Explorium Irelands National Sport & Science Centre

STEM

A fun, hands-on playdough session where toddlers can squish, roll, and shape their own creations. This activity encourages creativity, fine motor skills, and sensory play, while also giving little ones the chance to socialise, share ideas, and play together. The Playdough Session runs Mondays and Thursdays from 10-10.30am, and toddler tickets include 2 hours' exploration of Junior Explorium. All mums, dads, grandparents and caregivers are welcome to stay. Free car parking. Coffee shop on site.

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Contact: junior@explorium.ie
Address: Sandyford, Dublin, D18 N1F2, Ireland
