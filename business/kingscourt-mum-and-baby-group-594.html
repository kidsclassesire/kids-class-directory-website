Kingscourt Mum and Baby Group

Developmental

Kingscourt New Mum and Baby Support Group in Cavan welcomes new and expecting mothers to meet up, share experiences and create new friendships. It takes a village to raise a child, so why not join this group and meet some new friends over a cup of tea? The group also runs baby massage/reflexology classes at times. Location: Kingscourt Community Centre, Dunaree, Kingscourt, Co. Cavan. Time: every 2nd and 4th Tuesday of the month, 10-11.30am. Cost: €3 per family. Contact: Berniece on WhatsApp, 0871860352.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old
Address: Dunaree Lane, Dunaree, Kingscourt, Co. Cavan, Ireland
