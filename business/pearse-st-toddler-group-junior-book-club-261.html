Pearse St Toddler Group & Junior Book Club

Parent-and-toddler

Pearse Street Library offers lots of activities and groups for families. 📍Location: Pearse Street Library, 139 - 144 Pearse Street, Dublin 2 💶 Cost: €FREE 🧸Contact Details: 01 222 8430 / pearsestreetlibrary@dublincity.ie Parent and Toddler Group Toddler group featuring story-time, songs and rhymes facilitated by the wonderful Early Learning Initiative. Followed by playtime. Please register for this event ahead of time. --Each ticket covers 1 adult and 1 child. If you book and cannot attend, please cancel your ticket to release it to the waiting list ⏰Time: Every Tuesday and Friday morning from 10.30am. Suitable for children aged 8-12 years, Email to book a place and find out what is the book of the month. ⏰Time: Book club will be on monthly usually on Wednesdays at 5pm to 5.45pm (check with the library as dates/times change)
