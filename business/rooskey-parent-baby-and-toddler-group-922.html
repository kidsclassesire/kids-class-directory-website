Rooskey Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Roscommon - Also known as "Little Live Wires" playgroup 🌻 Rooskey Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Welcoming all mums, dads, carers, grandparents & childminders. New members from any area always welcomed. If you haven't been before there is no need to book just show up. Come along for a cuppa and a chat while the babies and kids play with lots of great toys (we also have high chairs and baby chairs for the babies). There is tea, coffee and biscuits offered. The group has great parties for holidays such as Halloween and Easter. Location: Rooskey Community Centre, Main St, Roosky, Co. Roscommon, N41 F9C2 Day/Time: Every Every Friday from 10.15 am to 12.15 pm. 💶 Cost: €3 per child

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Main St, Roosky, County Roscommon, Ireland
