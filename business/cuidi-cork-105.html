Cuidiú Cork

Developmental

Our events have become famous for helping parents find the tribe that will offer them support and encouragement along their parenting journey. Whether you are breastfeeding, bottle-feeding, staying at home or working outside of the house, you’re in the right place. From coffee mornings to playgroups, cafe meet ups to breastfeeding support, our events are an enjoyable way to socialise with other parents. We have regular parent, toddler, baby, breastfeeding info, and social meet ups throughout Cork. Whether you are a mum, dad, grandparent, or carer, you are very welcome to our events. Join us at one of our meet ups and enjoy making friends and connections with local mums and dads while letting the smallies make new pals. For up to date information on our events check out our website: https://www.cuidiucork.ie/cuidiú-cork-events
