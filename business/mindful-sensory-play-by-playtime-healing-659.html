Mindful Sensory Play by Playtime Healing

Parent-and-toddler

Mindful Sensory Play is a child-led session for parent and toddler to connect, meet new people, build developmental skills and foster their sense of curiosity and wonder! These sessions are designed to promote the early development of mindfulness concepts through present moment awareness and awareness of the five senses, and important life skill with research shows has lifelong benefits! These sessions take place at various locations around Cork, with regular sessions in Ballincollig, Clonakilty and Bantry.

Ages: 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
