Stuntworx Elite Gymnastics and Cheerleading

Gymnastics

Stuntworx Elite offers classes for recreational and competitive gymnastics and cheerleading, as well as ninja warrior classes on a recreational basis: Gymnastics Classes, Monday-Saturday; Cheerleading Classes, Monday-Saturday; Ninja Class, Friday. Parents drop their athletes and leave, or are welcome to watch from the small foyer area. Athletes require water and should wear clothing that is good for working out - no strings, no skirts and no jewellery.
