Clondalkin Library Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Clondalkin Library Baby and Toddler Group every Tuesday from 10-11.30am. It is self-led. Clondalkin Library will provide the space and toys, you provide the playtime! There is no cost to attend. Keep an eye on the library socials for fun family activies like story time, baby reflexology and more!!

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Clondalkin, Dublin, D22 XP03, Ireland
