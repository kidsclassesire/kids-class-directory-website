Outdoor Playgroup for toddlers to 10 years - Down to Earth

Parent-and-toddler

Rosscahil Parent and Toddler Group in Galway is also known as Little Twigs. They are an Outdoor Parent and Toddler group that focus on natural fun, following the seasons of free play, crafts, stories, songs a mud kitchen and a camp fire. The group is suitable up to preschool age. This group operates by signing up for a term so check out their website and socials to stay up to date. 🗓️⏰Time: Usually Wednesday 10.00am - 11.30am There is also Woodland Wildlings group for 5years to 10 years. Where the kids have fun in the woods, have free play time, games, seasonal crafts, learn bushcraft skills, campfire and foraging. This group operates by signing up for a term so check out their website and socials to stay up to date. 🗓️⏰Time: Usually Wednesday 3pm - 5.00pm Check out Down to Earth Forest School for More Information 📍Location: Brigit's Garden, Pollagh, Rosscahil, Co Galway 💶 Cost: €90 for 6 week term 🧸Contact Details: you can contact downtoearthgalway@gmail.com for any queries
