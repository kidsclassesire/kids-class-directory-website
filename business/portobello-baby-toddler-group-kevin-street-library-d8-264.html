Portobello Baby & Toddler Group - Kevin Street Library D8

Parent-and-toddler

Kevin Street Library offers 2 toddler groups which is always open to new members. Plenty of toys and books to choose from so your little one will have a great time. 📍Location: Kevin Street Library, 18 Lower Kevin Street, Dublin 8 💶 Cost: €FREE 🧸Contact Details: 01 222 8488 / kevinstreetlibrary@dublincity.ie Baby and Toddler Group We share toys, stories, and from time to time songs. All babies and toddlers are welcome. No need to book. Library assistant will be reading some stories in the children's library. It will be really laid-back so feel free to come and go as you please. ⏰Time: Every Wednesday from 10.30 to 11.30am..
