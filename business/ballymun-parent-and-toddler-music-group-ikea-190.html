Ballymun Parent and Toddler Music Group - IKEA

Parent-and-toddler

Ballymun Parent, Baby and Toddler Music Group is brought to you by IKEA. The hold two separate sessions based on your child's age. fun way to interact and inspire your child through play. Places are limited and FREE to IKEA Family members (you can sign up to the IKEA Family account for FREE). These spaces book up super fast so make sure you grab them if you really want to attend. 📍Location: IKEA, St Margaret's Road, Ballymun, Dublin 11, D11 FW18 (the back of IKEA Restaurant) ⏰Time: First Tuesday of every month - Toddler Music Class - Walkers to 5 years 10am-11am - Baby Music Class - Newborn to walker 11am-12pm 💶 Cost: FREE

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Balcurris, Dublin, D11 FW18, Ireland
