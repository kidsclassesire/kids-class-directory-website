Mullingar Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Westmeath Mullingar Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is also known as Oodles & Doodles is for parents/carers & their pre-schoolers. Toys to play with, refreshments for all to enjoy and singing, plus the opportunity to chat with & meet new people. Location: Mullingar Presbyterian Church Day/Time: Every Wednesday at 10.30am -12pm (during school term time) 💶 Cost: FREE per family

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: 086 8169316
Address: Mullingar, County Westmeath, Ireland
