Cabra Library - Toddler Group / Book Clubs and More

All are welcome to drop in. ⏰Time: Mondays and Thursdays from 10.30am-12pm Children's Story Time The Library staff will be reading some lovely stories for the kids so come along to relax and listen to some fun stories. Suitable for children aged 5-10. Children under 9 must be accompanied by an adult at all times during the event.
