Roscrea / Corville Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Roscrea Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Tipperary is a welcoming setting for adults and children alike. Mums, Dads, Grandparents and Childminders are all welcome to pop along with your little one. There are plenty of toys for kids to play with and a perfect opportunity for the children to interact with other children their age. The group operates in a pop in style, so no need to sign up for a term. 📍Location: Scoil Iosef Naofa, Corville, Cor An Bhile, Roscrea, Co. Tipperary 🗓️⏰Time: Every Thursday 9.30 - 10.30 (during school term time) 💶 Cost: FREE 📣 Contact Details: you can contact Lisa 086 1913383 / the facebook page for any queries
