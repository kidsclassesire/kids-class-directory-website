Clonmel & Cashel Parent and Baby Group

Parent-and-toddler

Mother and Baby Group in Cashel & Clonmel Ran by Community Mothers Programme. A programme designed for all Parents with children O – 5 years. Mother & Baby Group is an opportunity to meet other mums and babies with Community Mothers on hand to provide you with support and information. Clonmel Location: Clonmel Resource Centre, Kickham St, Clonmel Day/Time: Every Monday 2pm - 3:30 💶 Cost: FREE Cashel Location: Our Lady of Lourdes Hospital, Cashel Day/Time: Every Thursday 10am - 11:30am 💶 Cost: FREE

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months
Address: Cashel, County Tipperary, Ireland Clonmel, County Tipperary, Ireland
