Little Artists Ireland

Arts and crafts

Fun and creative weekly art classes for children, focusing on painting and pencil drawing. Each session helps children build confidence, creativity, and basic art skills in a relaxed and friendly studio. Time & location: every Saturday at 10am and 11.15am (each class is one hour), Art Studio, Clara, Co. Offaly. Open to children aged 4 and up, including teens. Caregivers are welcome to stay but don't need to remain for the full class. All materials are included.

Ages: 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old, 12 year old, 13 year old, 14 year old, 15 year old
