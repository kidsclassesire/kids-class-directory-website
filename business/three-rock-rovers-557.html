Three Rock Rovers

Hockey

Three Rock Rovers (TRRHC) runs one of Ireland’s most active youth hockey programmes, with over 350 boys and girls aged 5 to 16 training every weekend from August to March. With more than 16 hours of hockey each weekend, the club offers expert coaching in a fun, welcoming, and inclusive environment. | ​ | The youth section includes dedicated goalkeeping sessions with high-quality coaching and equipment. | ​ | With top-class facilities at Grange Road and a long-standing hockey tradition, Three Rock Rovers is a great place for young players to learn, grow, and enjoy the sport.
