Bloom Baby Classes Wicklow and South Dublin

Developmental

South Dublin locations: Sandyford & Dun Laoghaire. Wicklow locations: Bray, Greystones, Wicklow Town & Arklow. Classes suitable from 6 weeks up until when your little one begins to confidently walk! 🐛 Caterpillar Club . (6 weeks - 6 months) 🐛 A gentle and calm introduction to our musical sensory classes. Activities designed to aid all areas of baby development, with a strong focus on parent and baby bonding. Our Caterpillar club class awakens baby's first senses and stimulates in a fun, yet soothing way, using music, sensory props and lights. We end the class with our amazing chill parachute and a short guided baby massage to encourage a lovely long nap! 🐝 Busy Bees (6 months to 15 months/or when baby is confidently walking) A fun, lively musical baby development class that focuses on growing baby's confidence for the big world around them. Encouraging fine and gross motor skills, body awareness, early language skills & rhythm. Building on the gentle activities of Caterpillar Club, this session uses instruments and our amazing branded props to bring on their development in all areas. We have a new theme to explore each week with super cute dress up, props and themed music. During our theme time our caterpillar club babies can work on their tummy time & Busy Bees will get busy practicing social skills with their new Bloom pals, whilst exploring a variety of exploratory play and sensory heuristic treasure baskets. This is the perfect time for Mummies or Daddies to catch up and get to know one another.
