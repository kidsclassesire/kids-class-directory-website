Bloom Baby Classes Wicklow and South Dublin

Developmental

Classes are available in South Dublin (Sandyford and Dun Laoghaire) and Wicklow (Bray, Greystones, Wicklow Town and Arklow), suitable from 6 weeks old up until your little one begins to confidently walk. Caterpillar Club (6 weeks-6 months) is a gentle and calm introduction to our musical sensory classes, with activities designed to aid all areas of baby development and a strong focus on parent and baby bonding. The class awakens baby's first senses and stimulates them in a fun yet soothing way using music, sensory props and lights, ending with a chill parachute and a short guided baby massage to encourage a long nap. Busy Bees (6-15 months, or until baby is confidently walking) is a fun, lively musical baby development class that focuses on growing baby's confidence for the world around them, encouraging fine and gross motor skills, body awareness, early language skills and rhythm. Building on the gentle activities of Caterpillar Club, this session uses instruments and branded props to support development in all areas. A new theme is explored each week with dress-up, props and themed music: during theme time, Caterpillar Club babies can work on tummy time while Busy Bees practise social skills with their new Bloom pals, exploring a variety of play and sensory heuristic treasure baskets. It's also a chance for parents to catch up and get to know one another.
