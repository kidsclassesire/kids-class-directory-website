Longford Baby Massage

Developmental

Baby Massage in Longford is the perfect class to learn baby massage and all the benefits it can help with, including better sleep and better digestion. It's a perfect time to bond with your baby and meet other new parents too. Suitable for babies aged 7 weeks to 6 months (pre-crawling). IAIM registered instructor with 15 years' experience. Note: there is no social media for these classes, but they're usually posted on the Longford Community Mothers Facebook page. Location: The Family Centre, St Mel's Rd, Townparks, Longford (behind Longford Cathedral). Contact: ccorcoran@westcd.ie / 0860442354. Time: usually Thursdays at 11am (check when the next term starts). Cost: €25 for 4 classes.

Ages: 0-6 months
Contact: 086 044 2354
Address: Townparks, Longford, Ireland
