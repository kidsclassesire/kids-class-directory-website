Walkinstown Baby & Toddler Storytime / Book clubs

Parent-and-toddler

Walkinstown Library has great groups on offer for families all of which are free so why not pop in. Toddler Storytime Childrens story time with Ann is a fun out for your little one with includes stories and songs, they are bound to have a great time! No booking is required for this so please drop in and share in the fun! For babies to 2 years. ⏰Time: Every Tuesday 10.45 am - 11.30am we will be having our weekly Sensory Hour Have you tried out the Cubbie or the fabulous Tovertafel (Magic Table) in Walkinstown Library yet? Now is your chance to do so. My Sensory Hour for primary school children and their parents/carers. Chill out in our Cubbie, play with the games on the Tovertafel, explore our sensory toy box full of fun and educational toys, meet other parents and get advice from our friendly staff about books suitable for your child. ⏰Time: Every Tuesdays from 3.30pm-4.30pm. Children's Bookclub Open to members aged 8-11 children are encouraged to read their favourite books and discuss them with other members. We meet up to be inspired by what other have read, share our likes and dislikes and to have fun! ⏰Time: The Fourth Wednesday of each month at 3.30pm 📍Location: Walkinstown Library, Percy French Road, Dublin 12 💶 Cost: € FREE 🧸Contact Details: you can contact walkinstownlibrary@dublincity.ie for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old
