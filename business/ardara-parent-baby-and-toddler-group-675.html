Ardara Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Ardara Parent and Toddler Group Co Donegal is a fun and safe environment for mums,dad's,granny's, grandads or guardians to attend with the kids. Everyone is welcome to come along. Activities include messy play, arts and crafts, sports and fun and games for the little ones. There is tea and coffee for the adults, a great way to meet new people and have a chat while the kids play. The group is always welcoming new faces and with a drop in approach no need to feel pressure to attend every week. 📍 Location: Ardara Parish Centre, Ardara, Co Donegal ⏰Day/Time: Every Monday from 10.30 -12.00 (During School Term) 💶 Cost: €3 per family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact cosabeaga@hotmail.com for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
