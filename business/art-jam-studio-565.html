ART JAM STUDIO

Arts and crafts

Art Jam Studio Killarney is a new creative space for adults to attend one-off creative activities such as Paint & Sip and Sculpt & Sip, along with drop-in sessions during the day to paint your own pottery, mindful art, collage making, splash art, tote bag painting, etc. Also providing pottery classes, abstract art classes, and mindful & intuitive art classes. There will be a range of drop-in creative activities and classes - something for everyone, in a unique space in the centre of Killarney town.
