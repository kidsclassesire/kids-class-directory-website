Swimtastic.ie

Swimming

Swimtastic.ie is a leading provider of high-quality swimming lessons for babies, toddlers, and young children across Ireland. Our goal is to create a fun, safe, and nurturing environment where children can develop essential water skills, build confidence, and foster a lifelong love of swimming. Age groups and programmes: Parent and Child Programme, where parents/guardians attend class with their little ones; Baby Swim (3-12 months), a gentle, sensory-rich water introduction focusing on bonding, water confidence, and foundational skills; Toddler Swim (1-3 years), play-based learning to enhance water safety, movement, and early swimming techniques; Preschool Swim (3-5 years), small group lessons designed to build independence, stroke development, and breath control; and Junior Swim (5+ years), where parents/guardians do not need to get in the water - progressive lessons focusing on technique, endurance, and water safety skills. Swimtastic.ie operates in multiple locations across the Midlands, using warm, child-friendly pools, including in Laois, Carlow, Kilkenny and Kildare. Why choose Swimtastic? Expert, fully qualified instructors; small class sizes for personalised attention; warm, family-friendly swimming pools; a focus on fun, safety, and skill development; and flexible class options to suit busy parents.
