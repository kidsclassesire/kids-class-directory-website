Dunmanway Parent, Baby & Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Dunmanway Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Cork is hosted by Dunmanway Family Resource Centre. Are you a parent looking for a safe and welcoming venue, where your child can play, explore and connect? We love to see new faces and are always happy to share info on upcoming events that might be of interest or talk through the services we offer at the centre.
