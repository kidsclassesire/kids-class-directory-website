Santry Parent Toddler Group - Little Darlings Play café

Parent-and-toddler

Santry Parent and Toddler Group can be found at Little Darlings Play café. A Relaxed venue for mammies, daddies, grandparents and minders to explore with their Babies, Toddlers & Wobblers. Or come for a catch up with a friend and let the kids play while you can watch them and enjoy a coffee. There are activities included which really gets the kids to come together and promotes social interaction (between 11 to 11.30am). This group runs throughout the year and at times has additional classes running for school breaks.
