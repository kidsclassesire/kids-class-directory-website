SENSEable Tots

Developmental

SENSEable Tots are programmes of teacher-directed lessons which use sensory materials to help your children explore the use of their most important inborn instincts – the five senses of touch, smell, hearing, taste and seeing. These lessons will also encourage your children’s learning and development in accessible, fun ways – through songs, play, crafts and active games. The classes are approximately 40 minutes long each and are suitable for children between 6 months and 3 ½ years old. | ​ | Classes are held in locations in | Shankill | and | Greystones.
