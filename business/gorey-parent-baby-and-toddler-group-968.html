Gorey Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Wexford A friendly, relaxed atmosphere where parents, childminders or grandparents can come and meet others while their children play in a safe, well equipped and colourful playroom . It is an informal group which is run by parents themselves and supported by GYNG staff. Ages range from new-born babies up to school going age. Free to join, but donations for tea/coffee, refreshments are greatly appreciated.
