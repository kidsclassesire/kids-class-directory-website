Happy Cubs

Swimming

We love Happy Cubs! | Fun, confidence-building activities for your little ones. With swim classes and music classes across Dublin, Wicklow & Cork, they’re all about helping babies, toddlers & preschoolers develop life skills at their own pace, through play, songs and plenty of smiles. | Their | Swim Cubs | classes teach water safety, basic swim technique and confidence in the water, while | Music Cubs | gives kids aged as young as 3-6 months up to about 5-6 years a chance to explore rhythm, sounds and social connection with others. Whether your child is ready to splash or just love to move and sing, it’s a lovely way to bond, grow and have fun as a family. Make memories, one small splash or note at a time!

Address: Blackrock, Cherrywood, Kilmainham, Loughlinstown and Sandymount
