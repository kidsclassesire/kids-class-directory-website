Baby Massage & First Aid

Developmental

Calming Little Feet offers a 6-week course where you will learn infant massage and the benefits of baby massage, which builds bonding and connection, in a supportive, non-judgemental environment. Gain useful tips and support along the way from a paediatric nurse and mam to 4 children. Everything is provided, just come along with your baby. There are also monthly Daddy & Baby classes on a Saturday, for dads to enjoy some bonding time with baby, and monthly parent/grandparent first aid courses.

Ages: 0-6 months
