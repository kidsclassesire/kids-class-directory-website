Dolphin Swim School

Swimming

Dolphin Swim School has been a trusted name in Dublin’s swimming community for over 40 years, offering expert-led swimming lessons for children of all ages and abilities. | Founded by a former national champion swimmer, the school emphasizes water safety, confidence, and skill development in a nurturing environment. | With small class sizes and a low pupil-to-teacher ratio, each child receives personalized attention to thrive at their own pace. | Lessons are conducted by fully qualified, Garda-vetted instructors, ensuring a safe and supportive atmosphere. | Classes are held at multiple Dublin locations, including St. Augustine’s, Carmona School, Tullyvale Leisure Centre, and Islandbridge.
