Swanlinbar Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Swanlinbar Parent, Toddler and Baby Group in Cavan is a volunteer led group. The group runs afternoon and weekend groups which is amazing as there are so few across the country. The group is open to baby and toddlers but open to older siblings to tag along. Plenty of activities, free play time, snacks, stories and songs. They even have special events so stay tuned to the facebook group. You got to check out this group! 📍 Location: Church St Hall, Swanlinbar, Cavan ⏰Time: Every Second Wednesday 3.00-4.30 (during term time) AND ⏰Time: Every Second Saturday 10.00 - 11.30 (during term time) 💶 Cost: €3 per family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact samymac1@live.ie for any queries **Keep an eye on social media for when the group starts back up after school holidays as it does not always align with term time.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Contact: samymac1@live.ie
Address: Swanlinbar, County Cavan, Ireland
