Renmore Parent Baby & Toddler Group

Renmore Parent, Baby and Toddler Group in Galway. The group is always welcoming new people as it is a pop in group. Come along to meet mums, dads, childminders and babies to preschoolers. The group has plenty of toys for the kids and often hosts special guests or events throughout the year.
