Rathmines Baby & Toddler Group & Junior Book Club - Rathmines Library

Parent-and-toddler

Rathmines Library toddler group offers stories, sing songs and toys as a fun and safe place to meet up with babies to preschoolers. There is no need to book, just drop in. ⏰Time: Every Thursdays at 1pm **Keep an eye on social media for when the group may not be operating. Children's Book Club Provides a space for children aged 7-12 to gather and discuss books. Nicknamed the "Greedy Readers" love to read, and love to eat!
