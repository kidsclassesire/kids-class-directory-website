Bloom Baby Classes Midlands Ireland

Parent-and-toddler

Locations: Athlone, Edgeworthstown, Longford, Mullingar, Tullamore. We have two classes on offer; caterpillar club for babies aged 0-6 months and busy bees for babies aged 6-15 months. 🐛 Caterpillar Club (Birth - 6 months) 🐛 A gentle and calm introduction to our musical sensory classes. Activities designed to aid all areas of baby development, with a strong focus on parent and baby bonding. Our Caterpillar club class awakens baby's first senses and stimulates in a fun, yet soothing way, using music, sensory props and lights. We end the class with our amazing chill parachute and a short guided baby massage to encourage a lovely long nap! 🐝 Busy Bees (6 months to 15 months)🐝 A fun, lively musical baby development class that focuses on growing baby's confidence for the big world around them. Encouraging fine and gross motor skills, body awareness, early language skills & rhythm. Building on the gentle activities of Caterpillar Club, this session uses instruments and our amazing branded props to bring on their development in all areas. Our classes have a different theme every week for a whole year. During our theme time, we have fun props, dress up and music related to that weeks theme. Theme time is also a great opportunity for busy bees to interact with each other while exploring our props, heuristic treasure baskets and sensory lights. Our caterpillar babies can work on their tummy time while enjoying the sensory lights and themed props. This is also a great time for our bloom parents to catch up and get to know each other. Our classes run Tuesday: Mucklagh Community Centre, Tullamore Wednesday: Mullingar Shamrocks GAA Club Thursday: Drum Community Centre, Athlone Friday: St Mary's Community Campus, Edgeworthstown

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old
