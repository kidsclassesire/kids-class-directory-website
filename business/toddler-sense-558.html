Toddler Sense

Developmental

Toddler Sense is a world of sensory stimulation, play, fun adventures and learning for your toddler - the follow-on programme from the award-winning Baby Sensory. Classes range from 13 months to 4.5 years. Come and help Jennifer and Todd on their mission to learn all about Planet Earth through adventure play, songs, music, puppet shows, light shows, bubble shows and much more - a magical journey of imagination and learning about the world around us. Each week has a different theme, from Space to Under the Sea, to an Ugly Bug Ball and a trek in the Jungle, so there's plenty to see and do. All activities in the class are designed to work on a range of skills and developmental milestones, including hand-eye coordination, gross motor, fine motor, visual stimulation, emotional regulation, exploring the world around us, and social and emotional development, to name a few. With 3 locations in North County Dublin, find your nearest class on the website.
