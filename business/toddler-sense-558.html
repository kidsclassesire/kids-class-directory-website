Toddler Sense

Developmental

Toddler Sense is a world of sensory stimulation, play, fun adventures and learning for your toddler. Toddler sense is the follow on programme from the award winning Baby Sensory. Classes range from 13 months to 4.5 years. Come and help Jennifer and Todd on their mission to learn all about Planet Earth through Adventure Play, Songs, Music, Puppet Shows, Light Shows, Bubble Shows and much more! This is a magical journey of imagination and learning about the world around us. Each week is a different theme, from Space to Under the Sea, to an Ugly Bug Ball and a trek in the Jungle, so there is plenty to see and do! Toddler Sense explores it all! | ​ | All activities in the class are designed to work on a range of skills and developmental milestones.
