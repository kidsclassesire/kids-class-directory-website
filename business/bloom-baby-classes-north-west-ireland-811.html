Bloom Baby Classes North West Ireland

Parent-and-toddler

Bloom Baby Classes offer gentle, research-based baby development sessions designed to support bonding, early learning, and sensory exploration. Each week introduces new themes, songs, activities, and opportunities for connection with other parents and caregivers. Who Can Attend? Any primary caregiver is welcome — mum, dad, grandparents, guardians, or trusted caregivers. Siblings can attend if the class allows, please send me a message to confirm. Are Caregivers Required to Stay for the Duration? Yes. Caregivers must stay for the full duration of the class to support their baby, join in with activities, and ensure safety. Do Babies Need to Bring or Wear Anything Special? Comfortable clothing suitable for movement and sensory play A blanket or small mat for baby to lie on (optional) For Caterpillar Club: massage oil can be provided, or caregivers may bring their preferred baby-safe oil. 1. Caterpillar Club (6 weeks–6 months) A calm, nurturing class for younger babies focusing on early bonding, baby massage, gentle sensory play, and age-appropriate developmental activities. Perfect for pre-sitters. 2. Busy Bees (6-15 months) A more active, engaging class for babies who are sitting, moving, or exploring. Includes music, sensory play, early movement activities, and opportunities to practice emerging developmental skills in a safe environment. Location: Breffni FRC - Carrick-on-Shannon Times: Weekly sessions Tuesdays Busy Bees 10am-10.45am Caterpillar Club 11-11.45am & Busy Bees

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old
