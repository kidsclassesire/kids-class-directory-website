Do Your Dance Kids - Mountanville

Dance

🌟 Do Your Dance Kids – Dance, Explore, and Shine! 🌟 This one's for the older kiddos aged 7+ who are ready to take their dance skills to the next level! Our classes are jam-packed with exciting choreographed dances, cool dance techniques, and flexibility exercises, all squeezed into a fun-filled, one-hour session. Your child will get to explore a variety of dance genres, from hip-hop and jazz to contemporary, giving them a well-rounded experience of the dance world! 🕺💃 At Do Your Dance, all children are welcome—we’re a completely inclusive environment that celebrates individuality and creativity. If your child has additional needs, just let us know before class, and we’ll make sure they have the best experience possible. Whether they’re a seasoned dancer or stepping onto the dance floor for the first time, your child will find a supportive community, make new friends, and discover endless opportunities to grow. Let’s dance, explore, and shine together! 🎉 Location: Classes are held in the GP Room at Mount Anville Montessori Junior School. Wednesdays; 18:00 - 19:00 To find us: Enter the gates and follow the arrows up to the roundabout. You can park beside the astro fields. Then, come through the blue gates to the right of the roundabout and follow the astro turf to the double doors. The GP Room is right there - just follow the music! Parking: Parking is plentiful and free, so finding a spot is easy! During the Class: Parents are welcome to quietly watch in the GP Room or wait outside in the corridor. If you’re comfortable, feel free to drop your child off and come back at pick-up time. What to Bring: Children should wear comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. Please ensure your child brings a water bottle or drink. There’s a water fountain and bathrooms available in the centre. Arrival Time: Please arrive 10 minutes before class to get your child ready to dance. Can’t make it? No worries! If something comes up, just let us know via phone (0877007298) or email (doyourdancefitness@gmail.com) 24 hours before class. We can’t wait to see your child dance, learn, and have fun with us! 🌟

Ages: 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old, 12 year old
