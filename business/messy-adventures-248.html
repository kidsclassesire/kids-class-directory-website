Messy Adventures

Developmental

Play promotes physical development, as it encourages children to practise their fine and gross motor skills as well as their hand eye coordination. Children are given the opportunity to explore their senses by using their whole bodies and development these movements. 👉Our classes allow parents and carers to play one-on-one with their children. Play also encourages children to learn how to engage in activities independently as well as with other children. All vital skills for their development. We pride ourselves in providing classes which are fully inclusive and we warmly welcome all babies & children.
