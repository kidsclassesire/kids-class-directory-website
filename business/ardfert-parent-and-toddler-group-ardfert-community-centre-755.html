Ardfert Parent and Toddler Group - Ardfert Community Centre -

Parent-and-toddler

Join the Parent and Toddler Group in Ardfert Community centre on Wednesday's from 9:00am-11am where children adore the company and space to run around in a safe, secure environment. A small donation of 2 euro is given by all our parents each visit and it goes towards the funding for trips and outings such as pirate petes and swimming in Banna beach hotel, Crazy Caves in Castleisland and Kennedys pet farm. Of course we don’t forget the important things in life and have a supply of tea and coffee to hand at all times. It’s great to meet with people who are in the midst of the same problems, anything from weaning to potty training and more. Regular visits from the local public health nurse and visits from a physiotherapist and a life coach/parenting expert. Goals To help children to develop social skills at their own pace. Building a child's emotional confidence. Supporting children's imagination and creativity. Learning through role-play.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Address: 86H9+75 Ardfert, County Kerry, Ireland
