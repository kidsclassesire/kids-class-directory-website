Fundamental Football

Football

Our fun football classes for boys & girls aged 3-6 focus on learning the basics of football in a positive, energetic environment where kids can build confidence, make friends, and, most importantly, have fun. We are the only football classes in Cobh dedicated specifically to 3-6 year olds, focusing on fun & confidence, basic football skills, teamwork & friendships, and a safe, positive environment. Location: Bunscoil Rinn an Chabhlaigh, Cobh. New term starting 7th June. Time: 10am & 11am.

Ages: 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old
