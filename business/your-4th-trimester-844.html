Your 4th Trimester

Developmental

Life with a new baby can feel overwhelming — even when everything is “going well” 🤍 This 6-week 4th trimester course is designed to support you through those early weeks with your baby. Each session gently combines: ✨ Baby massage & yoga ✨ Feeding & newborn care support ✨ Babywearing guidance ✨ Postnatal recovery + nutrition ✨ A calm space to ask questions and feel supported You’ll leave each class feeling more confident, more connected, and less alone. 📍 The Crescent Shopping Centre, Limerick
