Limerick Library Toddler Group, Storytime & Art

Parent-and-toddler

Watch House Cross Community Library in Limerick has great activities on offer for families. To make it event better they are FREE! Below are some of the regular activities but make sure to stay up to date with the library during school holidays as there are usually fun activities organised. 📍Location: Watch House Cross Shopping Centre, Moyross, Limerick 📣 Contact Details: you can contact watchhousecrosslibrary@limerick.ie for any queries Parent and Toddler Group Parents, grandparents, care-givers, all welcome! It's a great opportunity to meet and chat with other adults while babies & toddlers play together with our lovely collection of toys, teddies, building bricks, games, board books & more... Library staff pop in around 11am for nursery rhyme fun. New members always welcome! 🗓️⏰Time: Every Friday between 10:30am and 12pm (during school term time) 💶 Cost: FREE Storytime Suitable for Ages 2-6 years. Listen and relax as the library staff select and read some lovely stories. All are Welcome. 🗓️⏰Time: Every Thursday 4pm 💶 Cost: FREE Arts & Crafts A fresh set of craft materials will be provided. Collect your art materials from the library desk and make something to take home, to display in the library or both. This is a self-guided activity so children must be accompanied and assisted by their grown-up. 🗓️⏰Time: Every Saturday afternoon - drop in anytime between 11am & 4pm 💶 Cost: FREE

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old, 12 year old
Contact: +353 61 557 726 · watchhousecrosslibrary@limerick.ie
Address: Watch House Cross Shopping Centre, Kileely Road, Kileely, Watch House Cross, Limerick, Ireland
