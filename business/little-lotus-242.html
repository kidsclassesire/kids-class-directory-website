Introducing our Kids' Summer Sessions! Calling all little explorers aged 4-8 years - get ready for a July packed with fun, creativity, new friends, and mindfulness, with three special daily sessions designed to spark joy and curiosity. July 1st: Adopt Your Own Unicorn or Dinosaur - choose a plush unicorn or dinosaur to stuff, and fill out an official birth certificate to take home. July 2nd: Teddy's Tea Party - bring your favourite teddy for a delightful afternoon, decorate a custom t-shirt for your stuffed friend, and enjoy a tea party with savoury/sweet snacks and cotton candy lemonade. July 3rd: Sensory Slime Lab - create your own custom slime, mixing in sparkles, colours, and a charm to make it perfect. Every session also features gentle yoga and mindfulness exercises to help your child feel centred and ready for the day's adventure. Price: €50 per child per class, or €130 for the full 3-day bundle (July 1st-3rd). Time: 10am-12pm. Ages: 4-8 years. Spaces are limited - secure your child's spot today: contact Lyndsey on 0834214811 or email littlelotusdublin@gmail.com
