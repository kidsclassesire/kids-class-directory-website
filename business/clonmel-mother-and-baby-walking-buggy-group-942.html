Clonmel Mother and Baby Walking/Buggy Group

Parent-and-toddler

The Wandering Mothers, mother and baby walking group is a group of mums who meet up for a walk and a chat with the babies. We walk down the blue way and back (About 5km altogether), and some stay back and grab a coffee or treat afterwards. ✨ NOTE - DAY/TIME MAY CHANGE ✨ If you are not in the what's app group pop me a message on Instagram so I can add you in or fill out the form in my bio. Being part of the group is handy as we do a poll the night before to figure out numbers, which determines if it's going ahead. 📍Location: Meet Outside Fetch Coffee, The Quay, Quay St, Burgagery-Lands West, Clonmel, Co.
