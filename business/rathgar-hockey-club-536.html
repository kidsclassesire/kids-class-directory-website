Rathgar Hockey Club

Hockey

Rathgar Hockey Club offers a fun and inclusive environment for girls from 1st class through secondary school and boys from U8s to U12s. With a focus on learning, teamwork, and enjoyment, the club welcomes both beginners and experienced players. Training takes place at The High School, with sessions on Mondays (6:30-7:30pm): 6th Class & Minor Girls, U12 Boys; Fridays (6:30-7:30pm): Senior Infants to 5th Class Girls, U8 & U10 Boys; and Sundays: blitzes and matches.

Address: Danum, 6 Zion Rd, Rathgar, Dublin 6, D06 YR68, Ireland
