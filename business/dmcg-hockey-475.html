DMcG Hockey

Hockey

DMcG Hockey runs specialised hockey camps and skills clinics in Dublin for young players who want to improve their technical game. Led by expert coaches, many with Leinster and Irish international experience, each session is tailored to help players develop core hockey skills and tactical understanding. | ​ | DMcG focuses on individual player development – enhancing stick work, control, and game sense through targeted drills and game-specific problem-solving. The goal is for every player to have fun, build confidence, and leave better than they arrived. | ​ | Many participants have gone on to represent Leinster and Ireland at underage levels. Camps are open to players of all levels who are eager to grow their game.
