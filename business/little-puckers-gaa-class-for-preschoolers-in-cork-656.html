Little Puckers - GAA class for preschoolers in Cork

GAA

Join Little Puckers in creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for all children to learn and grow through the joy of Gaelic Games(GAA). Little Puckers is a Fun & Inclusive Gaelic Games based program for children aged 1.5-5 years old. Little Puckers is committed to providing easy access to Hurling and Football for every child Parents are key stakeholders in their child's enjoyment at Little Puckers. You will be asked to play with your child for most of the session. These collaborative activities help to develop strong bonds with your child which will lead to a stronger relationship.
