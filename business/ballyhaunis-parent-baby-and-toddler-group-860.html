Ballyhaunis Parent, Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Ballyhaunis Parent and Toddler Group Co Mayo is a place for parents and guardians to bring their young children to interact with other kids of a similar age, while adults can catch up with each other. This non profit making scheme is run by the parents, and the entrance fee is purely to cover costs (hall rental, insurance) and provide refreshments. They run an active facebook group where you can message them queries or ask to join their WhatsApp group (highly recommended for local updates) as admins are only on Facebook when babies are napping. 📍 Location: Ballyhaunis Community Centre, Upper Main St, Carrownluggaun,Ballyhaunis, Co Mayo, F35 Y337 ⏰Day/Time: Every Wednesday from 10.00 -12.00 (during school term) 💶 Cost: €4 per Family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact s.d.cleary@hotmail.co.uk for any queries but best to check out their faceboook or watsapp group

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Contact: s.d.cleary@hotmail.co.uk
Address: Carrownluggaun, County Mayo, F35 Y337, Ireland
