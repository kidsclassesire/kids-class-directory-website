Flying Ducks

Sports

The Flying Ducks is a youth ice hockey and inline hockey club for boys and girls aged 5 and up, welcoming players from Dublin, Meath, Louth, and Kildare. The club offers both Learn-to-Play sessions for beginners and competitive teams from Under 10 to Under 18. Ice hockey training takes place from November to February on a temporary ice rink, while inline hockey training runs year-round at leisure centres in Dublin 15 and Gormanston, Co. Meath. Saturday training times: 09:15, Learn to Play & Development; 10:15, U13 & U15 Teams; 11:15, U18 & Senior Teams.

Address: Mountview Community Centre
