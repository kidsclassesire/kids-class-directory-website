Monaghan / Castleblayney Breastfeeding & Baby Group

Parent-and-toddler

La Leche League offers breastfeeding information & encouragement by facilitating general mother-to-mother support. They run two events in Monaghan which are open to pregnant and breastfeeding mothers. All babies and toddler are welcome.
