Whitethorn Equestrian Centre

Horse Riding

Our Riding lessons/Camps/Tiny Tot sessions cater for all ages and levels, from total beginners to advanced riders. Horse riding is a wonderful way to experience the outdoors and also has many mental and physical benefits. Riding has been proven to help with regulating emotions, and has sensory benefits making riders feel grounded and connected. For the advanced riders we offer jumping, and we regularly attend shows and bring our riders along 😊
