Bloomfield Health & Leisure Club

Swimming

Swimming Lessons Kids Swimming Lessons Mullingar (Members Only) Our goal is not only to teach your Kids to swim, but to help them develop an understanding of the water. We help build water confidence and learn basic swimming techniques with 40 minute lessons over a 6 week course. Kids Lessons run throughout the year for all abilities between the ages of 4 and 12. Lessons run for 6 weeks on Wednesday, Thursday & Saturday. Kids Club Kids Club runs from 5.30pm - 8.30pm on Saturdays during the School Term and everyday during the School Holidays. Suitable for Kids aged 4-10 years old. A €15 charge applies to all Kids availing of the Kids Club which includes Evening Tea (Main Course, Dessert & Squash)

Ages: 4 year old, 5 year old, 6 year old, 7 year old, 8 year old, 9 year old, 10 year old, 11 year old, 12 year old
