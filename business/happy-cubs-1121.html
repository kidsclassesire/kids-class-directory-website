Happy Cubs

Swimming

Fun, confidence-building activities for your little ones with Happy Cubs. With swim classes and music classes across Dublin, Wicklow & Cork, they're all about helping babies, toddlers and preschoolers develop life skills at their own pace, through play, songs and plenty of smiles. Their Swim Cubs classes teach water safety, basic swim technique and confidence in the water, while Music Cubs gives kids aged as young as 3-6 months up to about 5-6 years a chance to explore rhythm, sounds and social connection with others. Whether your child is ready to splash or just loves to move and sing, it's a lovely way to bond, grow and have fun as a family.
