Lisacul,Castlerea Parent, Baby and Toddler Group Roscommon 🌻 Lisacul Parent, Baby and Toddler Group is also known as Brightsparks playgroup. Brightsparks Parent and Toddler group is a fun filled group for parents to meet over coffee, chat and for kids to play, meet other kids and have fun. Drop in sessions are the norm so don't worry if you can't make a week. There is a facebook group for parents and carers to join too. Location: Lisacul Community Centre, Lisacul, Castlerea, Co. Roscommon Day/Time: Every Thursday morning from 11-1 (during term time) 💶 Cost: FREE

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old, 5 year old
Address: Lisacul, County Roscommon, Ireland
