Toddler Groups with additional Parenting Support in Dublin

Parent-and-toddler

Shanganagh, Loughlinstown, Ballyogan, Mounttown, Balally and Hillview have Parent and Toddler Groups provided by Southside Partnership. (contact the group to be referred in to the PEEP Programme) A friendly, weekly parent and baby/toddler groups. We use a lovely programme called PEEP, Parents Early Education Learning Together Programme, which helps us all understand about baby and toddler development, and what it is like to become a new parent. Your baby/toddler learns to play with you, their most important person, while also learning how to play alongside other very young children. We have free play, snack time, circle time, song and storytime, and you get to talk to other parents and the staff. The PEEP+ groups have been designed with you in mind! Anyone who feels a bit isolated, or excluded from other things, or wants to attend a smaller group (max 8 parents, 8 babies/toddlers), we have a lot of experience including and welcoming parents and babies and toddlers like you! 💶 Cost: € FREE per family 🧸Contact Details: you can contact 01 7060100 / lorraine.stewart@sspship.ie for any queries

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old
