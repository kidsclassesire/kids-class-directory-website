Sing and Smile with Aisling

Parent-and-toddler

A fun Music Baby and Toddler Parent Group promoting wellness and music for children. This is more than just singing and dancing, its helping them grow! Classes to develop musical education for all ages, encouraging social development, movement and mindfulness Locations: -- Dunshaughlin Dance, Dunshaughlin at 10am Monday mornings -- Ratoath Community Centre, Ratoath at 10.15am Wednesday mornings -- The Oak Centre, Dunboyne at 10.15 Thursday mornings Aisling runs extra classes, in The Venue, Ratoath, Co. Meath, adhoc Saturdays, at 10:15am & 11:30am. which can be found on her website and socials Classes can be booked on the website www.singandsmilewithaisling.ie

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old
