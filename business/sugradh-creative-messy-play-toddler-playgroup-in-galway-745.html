Sugradh Creative/Messy Play Toddler Playgroup in Galway

Parent-and-toddler

At Sugradh Creative Classes, each week the theme of our class centres on a much-loved children’s book. The Sensory Trays are specially curated for each child. The sensory tray content designed for the 18–36- month age range allows children a more beneficial experience of sensory activities at their own pace. During the 1-hour class, 10-15 minutes are dedicated to a special time for sharing toys. This facilitates the children engaging in social experiments within a safe environment, contributing to the development of their social skills. As a mother, it is important to me when designing the tray content that all products used in the sensory tray are made from non-toxic materials. Tuesdays - 10:30-11:30 am ( 1 hour ) Athenry - Kardio kids ( Raheen Woods Hotel) for the 18–36-month age range Tuam & Loughrea - Fridays

Ages: 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
