Tinahely Parent Baby and Toddler Group

Parent-and-toddler

Come along to our monthly Parent Baby ant Toddler Group at "The Farm Shop" Tinahely, Co Wicklow. On occasion the group is hosted by people like Claire from "Wellness by Meadows" who teaches Baby Massage and Baby Reflexology Classes. We will meet once a month 10.30-11.30 am in "The Farm Shop" cafe. Tea/ Coffee and Scone €5.95 Afterwards if parents would like to use "The Play Barn" fun facilities the cost is €7.50 per child +16months It will be a lovely way to meet other Parents, babies and toddlers in your area. Stay up to date with the group on social media.

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old, 4 year old
Address: Coolross, County Wicklow, Y14 V386, Ireland
