FREE Parent and Toddler Groups (Dundalk and Drogheda)

Parent-and-toddler

Join other parents and toddlers for a morning of fun. A great opportunity for toddlers to interact with their peers and parents to connect with others in the community! Every Wednesday morning (during the school term) from 10am - 11am. No booking required. Feel free to drop in. Dundalk Group Muirhevnamor Community Centre Drogheda Group St. Nicholas G.F.C , Rathmullan

Ages: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 1 year old, 2 year old, 3 year old
Contact: info.abc@lldc.ie
Address: Marshes Upper, Dundalk, County Louth, A91 W974, Ireland St. Nicholas G.F.C., Rathmullan, Drogheda, County Louth, Ireland
