Kangoo Jumps Galway

Parent-and-toddler

Kangoo Jumps Classes – Fun for Kids & Parents! Kangoo Jumps is a high-energy, low-impact workout that’s perfect for kids, parents, adults and caregivers! Using special rebound boots, this class improves balance, coordination, and cardiovascular fitness while reducing joint impact. It’s a fantastic way to stay active, have fun, and bond as a family. Bonding Families (Kids 6+ & Parents) ⏰Monday at 6pm 📍 Location: Renmore Community centre Adults Beginners: ⏰Time: Tuesday at 9:30 a.m. 📍Location : Renmore Community centre. ⏰Time :Friday at 7:30 p.m. Location: Oranmore Maldron Hotel Who Can Attend? Kids (6+) can join with a parent, guardian, or grandparent. Parents/caregivers (mum, dad, grandparents) are welcome to participate. Adults can also join beginner classes independently. Are Caregivers Required to Stay? Not at all, but definitely can participate. They can either participate in the session or bring child to class and go. What Do Kids Need to Bring or Wear? Comfortable workout clothes and long socks (to wear with the boots). Water bottle for hydration. Kangoo Jumps boots are provided—just bring your energy and enthusiasm! Join us for a fun-filled fitness experience that gets everyone moving! 🚀
