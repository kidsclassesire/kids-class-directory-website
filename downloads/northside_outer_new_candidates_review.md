## Data-quality notes across all 8 towns (Raheny, Donaghmede, Kilbarrack, Artane, Coolock, Santry, Finglas, Ballymun)

1. **Multi-venue chains are bundled into single DB rows, hiding real per-town coverage.** The clearest example: id=1134 ("Turtle Tots") lists roughly a dozen venues — including "Sports & Fitness Finglas Mellowes Road" and "Sports & Fitness Ballymun Ballymun Rd" — jammed into one row's address field with a single Cork eircode. This means Finglas and Ballymun already have a Turtle Tots presence that a per-town address match won't surface, and it should be split into venue-specific rows (same fix already flagged for Isabelle Ashe Dance in the South Dublin review). Little Kickers, Kenpo-affiliated karate clubs, House of Swag, First Swim, and Starcamp all follow the *correct* pattern instead — one row per physical venue — which is why this round's ⭐ finds (Little Kickers Meakstown, House of Swag Finglas, First Swim Santry, Coolock Kenpo Karate, Starcamp Ballymun, Sean Gilligan Artane) fit cleanly alongside their siblings.

2. **Suburb boundaries are genuinely fuzzy and operators market across them**, which produced several ⚠️-flagged or reclassified candidates this round: Sean Gilligan Performing Arts' "Kilbarrack" venue (Star Studios) sits inside Kilbarrack Shopping Centre, confirmed as Kilbarrack proper only after checking the postcode. Olympus Dance & Music School markets itself to "Dublin 5, Coolock, Artane" but its actual unit is on Greencastle Parade, Dublin 17 — placed under Coolock, not Artane. Rockfield Tennis Club sits on the Artane/Beaumont line. Splashtastic and Whitehall Colmcille GAA both market to Santry/Donaghmede as catchment areas while their actual venues (Priorswood, Whitehall) are outside the town being reviewed — excluded from both towns' tables for that reason, not duplicated.

3. **Address-text-only row counts undercount real coverage in areas with granular sub-suburb naming.** Finglas is the sharpest example: the DB already has toddler groups and a Turtle Tots venue recorded under "Finglas North," "Finglas South," "Finglas East," "Meakstown," and "Wellmount Road" rather than the literal string "Finglas" — so the "3 rows" starting count for Finglas materially understates what's already there. Worth checking whether Coolock/Artane/Santry have similar sub-suburb labelling before treating their "0 rows" as literal gaps.

4. **GAA/schoolboy-soccer club websites are the weakest sourcing category throughout.** Almost every GAA and junior-soccer academy in this file (Kilbarrack United, Naomh Barróg, St Paul's Artane, Parnell's, Bohemian FC, Finglas Celtic, Ballymun Kickhams, Starlights GFC) required third-party aggregators (Facebook, Instagram, ClubForce, local news) to confirm ages/schedule because the clubs' own sites either don't resolve, don't list a street address, or bury programme details off the homepage. Any of these rows should be spot-checked against the club's current season before insertion, since nursery/academy details (especially cost and exact days) change year to year and several sources cited above were from 2019–2024.

5. **Community/social-support programmes keep surfacing as false positives** and need active filtering out, not just omission: Kilbarrack Coast Community Programme's "Youth Matters" and Ballymun's Aisling Project both looked like activity providers in search results but are drug-prevention/at-risk-youth support services, not classes a parent browses and books — excluded from both towns on the same basis.
