Similarly, "Marino Library" (and its toddler group) sits at Marino Mart, whose official Dublin City Council address is given as "Fairview, Dublin 3" — listed under Fairview, not Marino. Both are flagged inline; worth a quick sanity check against the geocoded address before insertion, since the site's own town-matching is address-driven, not name-driven.
