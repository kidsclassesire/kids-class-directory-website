WSKF Karate Academy Skerries

Volunteer-run club teaching traditional Shotokan and sports karate, strength and fitness. Ages 5 upwards. Come down and try a free class any time - sit in and see what goes on, or join in straight away. All you need is loose-fitting trousers and bare feet. Parents/caregivers can stay and watch or leave for the class. We always have beginner special offers too. All our volunteer coaches have coaching and child protection qualifications and are Garda vetted. Classes: Saturday, 2:30pm Beginners, 3:30pm Juniors; Monday, 6:45pm Juniors; Wednesday, 6:30pm Juniors. Location: Skerries Community Centre, Skerries. Find out more at wskfskerries.ie

Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools

The Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools have classes in Tallaght (Old Bawn), Kildare (Maynooth & Naas) and Carlow. Our schools have produced World, European & National champions, and our students have won medals representing Ireland both home and abroad. We offer both traditional and sport Taekwon-Do classes to children and adults, with special classes for younger children that give them an introduction to the martial arts in a fun way. We offer special rates for families wishing to join together. All our instructors are Garda vetted, qualified and recognised by the Irish Martial Arts Council. The school's senior instructor is 8th Dan Master Stephen Doyle, who has worked as Irish national coach for over 20 years and is a former European gold medallist. Whether it's for fitness or self-defence, if you or your children are interested in joining, drop along for a free class or contact Stephen on 087-6986491 / info@masterdoyle.com
