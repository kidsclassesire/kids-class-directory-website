BabyGrá - Baby Massage & Yoga Cork

A space to slow down, connect, and nurture: a 5-week Baby Massage Course designed to help you relax, bond, and connect with your little one. You'll learn gentle massage techniques to support deeper, more restful sleep, ease tummy discomfort and colic, and strengthen your bond through loving touch. Each course includes organic baby massage oil, a parent's manual with all strokes and routines, and mats and a cosy setup. Location: Croí Wellness Studio, Blackpool, Cork.

Little Learners Holistics

A safe group for mothers to learn more about wellbeing for mothers and babies. Baby massage is more than just touch - it's bonding, confidence, connection, and community. Come join us for gentle moments with your baby and a supportive space for you. Led by an Infant Massage Instructor (IAIM), Mother and Baby Wellbeing Facilitator, and Kids Yoga Instructor. Locations: Forrest Hall, Mitchelstown; Ganapati School of Yoga, Mallow; Gealach Grá Yoga Studio Chair; and Fermoy Community Youth Centre.
