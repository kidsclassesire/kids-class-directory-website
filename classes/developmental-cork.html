Kate Cuddy Yoga

Yoga classes at Glounthaune Community Centre on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays, suitable for absolute beginners, more advanced practitioners, teenagers, and new mums with their babies. Benefits include improved flexibility and mobility, increased strength, better posture, and improved balance and coordination, along with relief for back, neck or joint pain. Yoga also supports mental and emotional wellbeing by reducing stress and anxiety, improving focus and mental clarity, boosting mood, and supporting better sleep. Longer term, it encourages a stronger mind-body connection, is accessible to all levels with gentle or challenging options, and offers the structure and social support of a regular class or home practice.

BabyGrá - Baby Massage & Yoga Cork

A space to slow down, connect, and nurture: a 5-week Baby Massage Course designed to help you relax, bond, and connect with your little one. You'll learn gentle massage techniques to support deeper, more restful sleep, ease tummy discomfort and colic, and strengthen your bond through loving touch. Each course includes organic baby massage oil, a parent's manual with all strokes and routines, and mats and a cosy setup. Location: Croí Wellness Studio, Blackpool, Cork.
