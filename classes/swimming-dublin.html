Summer Swimming Camps for Children

SwimCamp offers intensive swimming camps for children throughout the year at Castle Park School Swimming Pool in Dalkey, Dublin, for kids aged 5 and up who have some previous swimming experience. Sessions usually last 45 minutes each day. Camps are divided into three skill levels (Groups 1, 2 and 3) to suit different abilities, from beginners to more advanced swimmers, with special clinics also available for teens aged 13+. Children can be accompanied by any caregiver - parents, grandparents, or childminders - though it's best to contact SwimCamp directly to confirm whether caregivers need to stay for the duration of the class, and for guidance on what to bring; typically for swim classes, children should bring a well-fitting swimsuit, a towel, goggles (if preferred), and a swim cap (if required by the pool). Each camp consists of three 45-minute sessions on consecutive days, focusing on improving existing swimming skills, building confidence in the water, and teaching water safety. Experienced, certified coaches lead small groups to ensure personalised attention, in a fun and engaging learning environment that helps children develop essential swimming strokes, strength, and endurance. Camps are priced at €65 for the three-day session, with multiple dates and time slots available throughout July and August.

Aura Leisure De Paul on Navan Road, Dublin 7, offers a comprehensive and engaging swimming programme for children aged 4 and up. With free swim assessments available to determine the appropriate starting level, Aura Leisure De Paul ensures every child receives personalised instruction tailored to their needs.

Splashtastic

Splashtastic Swim School offers high-quality children's swimming lessons in North Dublin, catering to kids aged 3.5 and up. Operating exclusively on Saturdays at St Michael's House Leisure Centre in Belcamp, the school provides a structured, fun, and supportive environment for swimmers of all levels. Classes range from beginner levels like Star Fish, focusing on water confidence and basic skills, to advanced levels such as Orca, where children refine all four competitive strokes and build stamina. For children requiring additional support, Dolphin classes offer small group sessions with a 4:1 student-to-teacher ratio, accompanied by a parent or guardian. Splashtastic also provides one-to-one lessons tailored for children with special needs. With experienced instructors and a commitment to safety and enjoyment, Splashtastic is a trusted choice for families in areas including Portmarnock, Swords, Malahide, and Donaghmede. Due to high demand, classes often fill quickly, but a waitlist is available for interested families.
