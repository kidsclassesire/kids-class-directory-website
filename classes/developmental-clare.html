Baby Massage Class in Shannon Co Clare

Brand new Baby Massage Classes, led by a Certified Infant Massage Instructor, are starting in the Family Resource Centre in Shannon, Co. Clare - perfect for babies from birth to pre-crawlers. The term runs for 5 weeks, with classes usually lasting about an hour and a half, including time for tea/coffee and chats at the end. It's a great way for moms to socialise with other new moms, sharing tips and tricks. Classes are baby-led, so no need to worry if baby cries, needs a feed/change, or just needs a cuddle - these classes are extremely relaxed. Stay up to date on social media to hear about upcoming classes and locations.

Baby Massage Classes Newmarket on Fergus

Baby massage is a way of communicating with your baby through a nurturing and loving approach. Infant massage is a gentle medium, following a simple massage routine that's adaptable to each family's own needs and wants. In my infant massage classes, your CIMI (instructor) demonstrates the massage movements on a doll while you massage your own baby, guided step by step in a relaxed and friendly environment, so you can continue to massage your infant for years to come. Babies who are massaged tend to sleep better and get relief from growing pains, muscular tension, teething discomfort, gas and colic. It also increases relaxation and reduces stress levels by stimulating the anti-stress hormones oxytocin and serotonin, for both parent and infant - a wonderful way to spend time with your baby. Classes run over 5 weeks. Stay up to date with new terms through our socials. Location: Harmony House, Newmarket-on-Fergus. To secure a place, contact Siobhan on 086 855 2307. With nearly a decade of teaching baby massage, you can be confident you're in safe hands.

Sense of Wonder Sensory Play

Welcome to Sense of Wonder Sensory Play. Our sensory play sessions are more than just fun - they're a powerful way to support your child's development and learning. Through hands-on exploration, babies and toddlers develop essential fine motor skills, hand-eye coordination, and problem-solving abilities. These sessions also stimulate cognitive growth, encourage language development, and build social skills through interactive play.
