Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools

Martial Arts

The Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools have classes in Tallaght (Old Bawn), Kildare (Maynooth & Naas) and Carlow. Our schools have produced World, European & National champions, and our students have won medals representing Ireland both home and abroad. We offer both traditional and sport Taekwon-Do classes to children and adults, with special classes for younger children that give them an introduction to the martial arts in a fun way. We offer special rates for families wishing to join together. All our instructors are Garda vetted, qualified and recognised by the Irish Martial Arts Council. The school's senior instructor is 8th Dan Master Stephen Doyle, who has worked as Irish national coach for over 20 years and is a former European gold medallist. Whether it's for fitness or self-defence, if you or your children are interested in joining, drop along for a free class or contact Stephen on 087-6986491 / info@masterdoyle.com

Ready Steady Play Midlands

Developmental

Our child development experts and play leaders create a unique experience weekly, encouraging early learning and development. In class we pride ourselves on forging a sense of community where parents and caregivers build personal connections and lifelong friendships, with classes throughout the Midlands. Through socialisation, your child will explore and discover the world around them. Our classes are great fun for parents, caregivers and children alike to share together in the vital first five years. Each week you and your little one will go on a new play and musical adventure into the rainforest, under the sea, practising circus skills, and many more, with over 30 themes to explore during the year. Our Sensory Baby Play classes are suitable from newborn to pre-walkers, while our Toddler Play classes are suitable for pre-walkers to 3.5 year olds. Classes run 6 days a week across the Midlands: Athlone, Mondays & Saturdays; Mullingar, Tuesdays & Wednesdays; Portlaoise, Wednesdays & Thursdays; Tullamore, Thursdays & Fridays; Naas, Fridays.
