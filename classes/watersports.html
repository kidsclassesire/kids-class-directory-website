Watersports Classes in Ireland

Explore 4 Watersports classes and activities for kids across Ireland. Watersports classes are most widely available in Dublin.

Skerries Sailing Club

Dublin

Skerries Sailing Club (SSC) located off the coast of north County Dublin, offers a wide variety of classes. SSC Clubhouse Dinghy and keelboat racing for adults and juniors takes place up to three times weekly in season. SSC prides itself on its friendly atmosphere. Newcomers to sailing are warmly welcomed.

Surfdock

Based at Grand Canal Dock, Surfdock offers a unique combination of activities including paddleboarding, kayaking and windsurfing for beginners. They offer intermediate paddleboarding classes and trips in and around Dublin, as well as intermediate windsurfing in the Malahide Estuary. Their group sessions are popular for kids' birthday parties from April to October. They also run a summer camp for 8 to 16 year olds, with an opportunity to try everything from kayaking, sailing, windsurfing, wakeboarding, paddleboarding and ringo rides. In addition, they run SUP Yoga classes with Ireland's only qualified SUP Yoga instructor, Alexa von Zett of Dublin SUP Yoga Fitness and Urban Yoga Dublin.

The Irish National Sailing & Powerboat School

Based in Dun Laoghaire Harbour, The Irish National Sailing & Powerboat School has been teaching people on sailing courses and powerboating courses since 1978. They provide all essential gear, boats and buoyancy aids. Their highly experienced instructors are fully insured and have an excellent safety record.

The National Yacht Club

Based in Dun Laoghaire, the National Yacht club offers sailing classes to kids as young as 6. There are also many other non sailing activities taking place within the Club. Each year the NYC run two week sailing courses throughout the summer. These are open to children of members between the ages of 8 to 18 years old. The Club offers all ISA qualifications from Start Sailing to Pre Instructor level.
