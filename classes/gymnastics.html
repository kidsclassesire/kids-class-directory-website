Muckross Gymnastics Club

Dublin

MPGC provides a range of gymnastics classes from Preschool Gymnastics (3+) through to Teen classes (12+), and open play for babies to preschoolers. The majority of our gymnasts take part in our one-hour-per-week Recreational Classes, which run all week at our venue in Sandyford Business Park, Dublin 18. Artistic Gymnastics Classes focus on fun, learning new skills, and gaining strength, flexibility and discipline that helps our gymnasts in all aspects of their lives, training on asymmetric bars, balance beam, vault and floor, as well as learning new skills on the trampoline and AirTrack. Extra Rec is perfect for any of our Recreational gymnasts (1 hour or 1.5 hour per week groups) who would like a second class to build on their skills. Ninja Class is for boys and girls who love to run, jump and climb - a parkour-style class with lots of obstacle courses and fun challenges. Trampoline Class is for girls and boys who love to jump, using our Olympic-size trampoline, AirTrack, mini tramp and more. Tots Open Play is open to crawlers up to age 4 (pre-school boys and girls), offering parent-supervised play in our gym with lots of room to run, bounce, swing and climb - it isn't coach-led, but we're on hand to help and play, and you can drop in anytime and stay for the whole session or just a little bit. Sibling discounts are available, and don't forget to ask for our loyalty card.

Arabian Gymnastics Club

Dublin

Arabian Gymnastics Club offers recreational and competitive gymnastics classes for boys and girls of all ages. Located in a 10,000 sq ft facility in Baldoyle, Dublin, the club is equipped with a range of gymnastics equipment, including sprung floors, AirTracks, bars, beams, vaults, trampolines, and resi mats. Established in 2005 as Dublin's first full-time gymnastics facility, Arabian Gymnastics provides structured training in artistic gymnastics, focusing on skill progression and fitness development. Trojan Gymnastic Academy

Dublin

Trojan Gymnastics Academy offers 3 fun opportunities to play and learn. Bambinos Play Session (closed July & August, reopening Tuesday 9th September 2025) is a great way to let loose, have fun, and connect with your child - an open play session in which your little one can roam freely, exploring the gym by running, climbing, jumping, balancing and more, and socialising with other children. In these sessions, children must be supervised at all times by a parent, as an instructor will not be present. Tiny Tumblers gymnastics classes are for two-year-olds, lasting 30 minutes and suitable for both boys and girls - these sessions help develop your child's resilience and self-confidence, with experienced staff bringing a friendly, fun and caring element. The structured lessons make use of preschool gymnastics equipment, teaching aids, and a range of miniature obstacle courses, perfect if they have a lot of energy to burn off. Tumble Tots gymnastics classes are ideal for children aged 2.5 to 4.5, with small group classes lasting 45 minutes, suitable for both girls and boys. Tumble Tots classes help develop your child's balance and strength, and increase flexibility and improve coordination, key skills for supporting your child's growth. The obstacle courses, gymnastic rolls and balancing work teach your child how to work independently as well as in a group - a great way to reduce separation anxiety and encourage your child's emotional development.
