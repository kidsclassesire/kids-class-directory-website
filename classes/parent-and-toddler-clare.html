Maia Purposeful Play

Discover a world of child-led play, learning and meaningful engagement for your little ones at Maia Purposeful Play. Our mission is to nurture curious minds and foster holistic development through child-led, interactive and carefully planned play experiences. From sensory play to imaginative play and creative exploration, we offer a range of thoughtfully curated experiences that spark imagination, encourage critical thinking, and promote social skills. Our carefully designed activities are aimed at enhancing children's cognitive, emotional, and physical development while making every moment a memorable adventure. Maia Purposeful Play classes are child-led, meaning children choose their play path and can move freely between the play areas and resources. This empowering approach not only fosters independence but also allows children to unearth their passions, cultivate curiosity, and create their own meaningful play experiences. During each 60-minute session, parents/caregivers and children are invited to embark on this play journey together.

Stories & Stations

Stories & Stations is run by primary teacher and toddler mum, Maebh. It begins with child-led play at the fun, educational play stations to aid your toddlers' development and feed their curiosity, their senses and their imagination. Stories and songs are led by Maebh at the Story Station for the last 20 minutes of the class. The class ends with some bubble fun. This class is on at 10am for an hour twice a month generally on a Friday (updates on class dates are on storiesandstations Instagram page). Classes are held at Quin Community Centre, Co Clare in The Abbey Room. The class is for 1-3 year olds but we have had some babies of 6 months - 12 months join us from time to time. It is recommended you contact Maebh if hoping to book a child in under the age of 12 months and she will ensure there are appropriate play stations for your little one. Caregivers are expected to stay for the duration of the class and are responsible for their child / children to ensure their safety. Dress your child in clothes that can get dirty as there will be sensory play stations which may include paint, water, sand and playdough.
