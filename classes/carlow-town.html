Carlow Parent & Toddler Messy Play Group - Visual Carlow

Parent-and-toddler

A series of Early Years workshops at VISUAL for children aged 1-4 years and their adults, exploring the galleries and exhibitions and engaging in a sensory and messy play activity each week. The workshops are led by artist Marian Balfe. Children should wear messy-friendly clothes. Must be booked in advance - tickets can be purchased online, in person, or over the phone at the box office. Note: please check their website and socials to keep up to date with the toddler group activities, as they may change throughout the year.

Dance Republic Tiny Dancer

Dance

Tiny Dancer Classes (ages 1-3): for over a decade, our beloved Tiny Dancer programme has brought the magic of music, movement, and dance to young children. These engaging, laid-back sessions are crafted for toddlers and their parents to share in the fun together. Each 50-minute class is packed with lively movement to well-known songs, from nursery rhymes to favourites that both kids and parents adore. Through simple dance steps, rhythm activities, and imaginative movement, tiny dancers build coordination, confidence, and a passion for music. There's a brief mid-class break with a small snack for the children and a cup of tea for parents, a moment to relax, connect, and enjoy the warm community that has blossomed around Tiny Dancer over the years. Our Tiny Dancer classes are a lovely introduction to dance - gentle, joyful, and filled with cherished moments for you and your little one.

Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools

Martial Arts

The Master Doyle Taekwon-Do Schools have classes in Tallaght (Old Bawn), Kildare (Maynooth & Naas) and Carlow. Our schools have produced World, European & National champions, and our students have won medals representing Ireland both home and abroad. We offer both traditional and sport Taekwon-Do classes to children and adults, with special classes for younger children that give them an introduction to the martial arts in a fun way. We offer special rates for families wishing to join together. All our instructors are Garda vetted, qualified and recognised by the Irish Martial Arts Council. The school's senior instructor is 8th Dan Master Stephen Doyle, who has worked as Irish national coach for over 20 years and is a former European gold medallist. Whether it's for fitness or self-defence, if you or your children are interested in joining, drop along for a free class or contact Stephen on 087-6986491 / info@masterdoyle.com
