All Court Tennis

Dublin

All Court Tennis, based at the Railway Union Sports Grounds in Sandymount, Dublin 4, offers dynamic, confidence-building tennis coaching for children of all skill levels. Their programmes emphasize not just athletic growth—improving coordination, agility, balance, and focus—but also personal development through lessons in sportsmanship, problem-solving, independence, and teamwork. With a warm, inclusive philosophy, they aim to nurture not only skilled players but well-rounded individuals who love the game for life.

LC Tennis Academy

Dublin

LC Tennis Academy offers a 30-minute Junior Intro Class for children new to tennis. For just €10, kids get a taste of the fun drills and games used in regular coaching, while parents can meet the coaches and ask questions - this session helps determine the best group for your child's level and interests. After the intro class, children can join the Junior Coaching Programme, which includes 11 weeks of coaching, 1 Team Tennis Event to practise skills in a friendly competition, 1 Family Social Event to enjoy tennis together, and 1 court booking for extra practice.

Lansdown Lawn

Dublin

Lansdowne LTC runs a Junior Tennis Programme alongside the school year, offering group coaching from September to June and holiday camps during school breaks. The programme welcomes all levels, from complete beginners to experienced young players. Located in Ballsbridge, the club features 10 all-weather floodlit courts, a practice court, and a modern clubhouse with excellent viewing facilities. Lansdowne also hosts a variety of social and competitive events throughout the year, including the Leinster Junior Open Championships and other fun tournaments that help juniors stay active and involved in the sport.

Parks Tennis

Dublin

Parks Tennis Ireland is a non-profit-making company in operation for 40 years, run by 28 volunteers and supported by various organisations, such as the national governing body Tennis Ireland, Sport Ireland, and various local authorities. The aim is to provide tennis at a very low cost to children not otherwise exposed to the game, from all social and ethnic backgrounds, during the long summer holidays. The total audience is over 250,000 through the distribution of application forms, with over 20,000 children taking part in the programme annually.

Donnybrook Lawn

Dublin

Donnybrook Lawn Tennis Club offers a welcoming and inclusive environment where children of all ages and skill levels can enjoy and improve their tennis in a fun, relaxed setting. The Junior Programme supports both social and competitive play through regular events like Super Saturdays, Round Robin tournaments, Play & Pizza nights, and inter-club matches. Additional sessions are organised as needed, including match practice ahead of tournaments or league play. During school breaks, Donnybrook runs junior tennis camps in February, Easter, and summer. Rathgar Tennis Club

Dublin

Rathgar Tennis Club offers a fun, safe, and supportive environment for children and teenagers of all ages and skill levels. Junior members can enjoy year-round coaching, make friends and stay active. Led by coach Jamie Pilkington and a dedicated team, the Junior Programme follows the school calendar with three terms of group lessons each year. Using the ITF Tennis 10s approach, younger players benefit from short courts and low-bounce balls to help them learn effectively. Lessons are tailored by age and ability, and older juniors can join one of three squad groups for additional coaching and up to eight hours of weekly training.

Stratford Lawn

Dublin

Stratford LTC offers year-round junior coaching on four floodlit all-weather courts, with access to four grass courts during summer. Coaching is available for children aged 5 and up, with lessons grouped by age and skill level. Classes follow the school year in 6-week blocks, focusing on technique, drills, and gameplay. Each 60-minute session uses the ITF Tennis 10s system, with red, orange, and green balls to support skill development. Summer camps replace weekly lessons during school holidays. All coaches are experienced, Garda vetted, and trained in child safeguarding.
