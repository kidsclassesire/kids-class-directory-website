All Court Tennis

Tennis

All Court Tennis, based at the Railway Union Sports Grounds in Sandymount, Dublin 4, offers dynamic, confidence-building tennis coaching for children of all skill levels. Their programmes emphasize not just athletic growth—improving coordination, agility, balance, and focus—but also personal development through lessons in sportsmanship, problem-solving, independence, and teamwork. With a warm, inclusive philosophy, they aim to nurture not only skilled players but well-rounded individuals who love the game for life.

Railway Union Hockey Club

Hockey

Railway Union Hockey Club has a long-standing tradition of family and community values, earning its reputation as 'the family club' in Irish hockey circles. The junior section is open to boys aged 5+ and girls from 1st class (age 7+), offering a supportive and fun environment to learn and enjoy hockey. With a strong focus on playing the game the right way, the club fosters respect, teamwork, and a lifelong love of the sport.

Happy Cubs

Swimming

Fun, confidence-building activities for your little ones with Happy Cubs. With swim classes and music classes across Dublin, Wicklow & Cork, they're all about helping babies, toddlers and preschoolers develop life skills at their own pace, through play, songs and plenty of smiles. Their Swim Cubs classes teach water safety, basic swim technique and confidence in the water, while Music Cubs gives kids aged as young as 3-6 months up to about 5-6 years a chance to explore rhythm, sounds and social connection with others. Whether your child is ready to splash or just loves to move and sing, it's a lovely way to bond, grow and have fun as a family.

NPAS

Drama

The National Performing Arts School is located in the Docklands and Sandymount, and is open to students aged from 2 to 22 years old. Drama/acting classes include dramatic play, story enactment, imagination journeys, improvisation, theatre games, music, and dance - 'let's pretend' is the norm in creative drama class, not just a child's game. A show is performed in the Olympia Theatre every two years.

PlayAct Drama School

Drama

PlayAct Drama School offers dynamic and creative drama workshops where kids and teens can build confidence, express themselves, and make new friends. Through acting, writing, and creating props, students develop social skills and learn to communicate in a fun and relaxed environment. The workshops are designed to meet students where they are, focusing on their interests and strengths to help them grow and explore their creativity - it's all about adventure, discovery, and learning through play.
