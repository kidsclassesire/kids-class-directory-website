Westwood Club

Swimming

West Wood Club offers expert-led children's swimming lessons across three premier Dublin locations: Clontarf Road, Leopardstown, and Westmanstown. With over 25 years of experience, their structured programme caters to all ages and abilities, guiding children through nine progressive levels, from first splashes to mastering strokes like butterfly and breaststroke. Classes are divided by ability rather than age, ensuring each child receives personalised instruction in small groups led by qualified and experienced teachers. Free assessments help place your child at the right level, and all lessons adhere to Swim Ireland guidelines. With a focus on fun and safety, West Wood Club provides a supportive environment where children can build confidence and enjoy their swimming journey.
