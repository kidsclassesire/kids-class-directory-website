Muckross Gymnastics Club

Gymnastics

MPGC provides a range of gymnastics classes from Preschool Gymnastics (3+) through to Teen classes (12+), and open play for babies to preschoolers. The majority of our gymnasts take part in our one-hour-per-week Recreational Classes, which run all week at our venue in Sandyford Business Park, Dublin 18. Artistic Gymnastics Classes focus on fun, learning new skills, and gaining strength, flexibility and discipline that helps our gymnasts in all aspects of their lives, training on asymmetric bars, balance beam, vault and floor, as well as learning new skills on the trampoline and AirTrack. Extra Rec is perfect for any of our Recreational gymnasts (1 hour or 1.5 hour per week groups) who would like a second class to build on their skills. Ninja Class is for boys and girls who love to run, jump and climb - a parkour-style class with lots of obstacle courses and fun challenges. Trampoline Class is for girls and boys who love to jump, using our Olympic-size trampoline, AirTrack, mini tramp and more. Tots Open Play is open to crawlers up to age 4 (pre-school boys and girls), offering parent-supervised play in our gym with lots of room to run, bounce, swing and climb - it isn't coach-led, but we're on hand to help and play, and you can drop in anytime and stay for the whole session or just a little bit. Sibling discounts are available, and don't forget to ask for our loyalty card.

First Swim

Swimming

First Swim is Ireland's first purpose-built baby and toddler swim school, located in Sandyford, Dublin 18. Designed by parents for parents, it offers a warm, nurturing environment where little ones aged 10 weeks to 3 years can develop water confidence and essential safety skills. Classes are led by certified instructors in a 33°C pool, ensuring comfort for babies and toddlers alike. With spacious changing rooms, ample buggy parking, and an on-site café, First Swim provides a stress-free and enjoyable experience for families. Their structured programmes focus on bonding, sensory play, and building a lifelong love of the water.
