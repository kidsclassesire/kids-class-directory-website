Fundamental Movement Academy

Louth

Fundamental Movement Academy (FMA) helps children develop the essential movement skills that form the foundation for all sports and physical activity. Our fun, engaging sessions focus on: 🏃 Running 🦘 Jumping 🎯 Throwing 🤲 Catching ⚖️ Balance 🔄 Coordination 💪 Agility ⭐ Confidence and teamwork Each 6-week block focuses on a different fundamental movement skill through games, challenges and age-appropriate activities. Every child receives an FMA Movement Journal to track their progress, complete weekly home challenges and celebrate their achievements. Class Times • Ages 5–7 years: Wednesdays, 4-4:50 • Ages 8–12 years: Wednesdays, 5-5:50pm 📍 Location: Louth, Dundalk Who can attend? Children aged 5–12 years. Parents/guardians are welcome to stay or return at the end of the session. What to bring Children should wear comfortable sports clothing, runners/trainers and bring a bottle of water. 🎉 Trial sessions available 👨‍👩‍👧 Sibling discount available Move Better. Play Better. Fitclub

Wicklow

Fitclub & Playball offer a unique experience for parents and children combining movement and play for all the family. This is a great way to stay active together. Classes start with a warmup suited to all ages and after that the class is split. 👉 Parents go outside and take part in a circuit style fitness class while children stay inside and do a fun playball class with a different coach. Playball is a great way for children to learn the movement and skills required for all sports when they get older. ⌚Fitclub runs every Monday and Wednesday from 4:30-5:30pm 📍It takes place in St. Patrick’s national school in Greystones. ANYONE above the age of 3 is welcome to try out our class. All adults are welcome, you do not need to be a parent to try out fitness classes! 💦Just bring a bottle of water and get ready to sweat!
