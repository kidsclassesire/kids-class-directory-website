Harmony Music

Music

Harmony Music is a music school in Ballincollig. We have been running lessons in the Carraig Centre since 2020 and have since added lessons in Gaelscoil Uí Ríordain. We offer 1:1 instrumental lessons in piano, cello, voice, guitar, bass guitar, violin and drums as well as group lessons for singing, guitar, Suzuki piano and Music Explorers. We teach adults, teenagers and children aged 4+ of all levels in various music styles. Our focus is to make progress while enjoying the process. We give our students the opportunity to take part in concerts, complete RIAM grade exams, or to take a student centered approach by taking into account the level, style and personal musical interests of the individual student. We believe its important to enjoy music while progressing and keep enjoyment of music in focus during lessons.

Woodworking Camps

Camps

Give your child the chance to create, explore, and build with confidence in our engaging Woodworking Camps designed especially for primary school children. Led by a qualified post-primary woodwork teacher, these hands-on camps introduce children to the exciting world of woodworking in a safe, supportive, and fun environment. Throughout the week, participants will learn how to use real tools correctly, understand basic woodworking techniques, and bring their own projects to life—from simple creations to pieces they can proudly take home and use. Our camps are designed to do more than just teach skills. They aim to: Build confidence and independence through practical, achievable projects Encourage creativity, problem-solving, and imagination Develop fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination Foster a sense of pride and accomplishment in making something from scratch Promote teamwork, patience, and focus in a relaxed setting Safety is at the heart of everything we do. Children are closely supervised at all times and guided step-by-step, ensuring they feel secure while learning new skills. Whether your child loves working with their hands or is trying something new, this camp offers a rewarding experience away from screens—one where creativity and craftsmanship take centre stage. Spaces are limited to ensure individual attention, so early booking is recommended. Let your child discover the joy of making something real.
