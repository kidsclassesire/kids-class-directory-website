Dolphin Swim School

Swimming

Dolphin Swim School has been a trusted name in Dublin's swimming community for over 40 years, offering expert-led swimming lessons for children of all ages and abilities. Founded by a former national champion swimmer, the school emphasises water safety, confidence, and skill development in a nurturing environment. With small class sizes and a low pupil-to-teacher ratio, each child receives personalised attention to thrive at their own pace. Lessons are conducted by fully qualified, Garda-vetted instructors, ensuring a safe and supportive atmosphere. Classes are held at multiple Dublin locations, including St Augustine's, Carmona School, Tullyvale Leisure Centre, and Islandbridge.

Monkstown Hockey Club

Hockey

Monkstown Hockey Club's Academy is home to over 650 young players, with around 200 boys and 450 girls, aged 6 to 16. With a strong focus on fun, skill development, and team spirit, the Academy has been a core part of the club for over 50 years, creating a pathway from junior hockey to senior success. Training sessions are held across multiple pitches, and teams take part in regular matches and blitzes, following the IHA's Hooked for Life model. The Academy prioritises small-group coaching, player progression, and, most importantly, a safe and enjoyable environment.
