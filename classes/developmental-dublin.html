Language Explorers is Mother Tongues' programme, designed for children aged 3 to 6 and their families. Facilitated by our specially trained multilingual artists, the Language Explorers workshops create a welcoming space for families from all linguistic and cultural backgrounds. Each session invites children and their adults to connect with their language(s) through storytelling, play, visual arts, music and movement. Our artists create a space full of imagination and creativity, in which speaking and listening to languages is fun and something to be proud of. Families take part in high-quality artistic activities while sharing and exploring the diversity of languages in the room, inspiring multilingual families and those curious about other languages and cultures. Workshops are delivered in English, and families are warmly encouraged to bring any and all languages they speak. Whether your child is fluent, just starting to learn, non-speaking, or communicates in a different way, this space is for you. Messy Fun

Messy Fun is a sensory play class designed especially for babies and toddlers that gives children the opportunity to experience a wide range of sensory experiences. Prepared solutions such as fingerpaint, slime and gloop give children a wonderful opportunity to experiment with different textures and materials. During messy play, babies and toddlers develop eye-hand coordination and fine motor skills - what looks like a mess on the surface is truly a learning experience for your child. For adults, messy play can be a lot of work and feel stressful, but early childhood education experts say that play is the work of the child, especially for babies and toddlers - don't be afraid of the mess, roll up your sleeves and enjoy some messy play time with your little one. Keep an eye on Messy Fun's social media pages for themed events throughout the year (Halloween, Easter etc.).

National Gallery of Ireland

Admission is free. The National Gallery of Ireland, right in the heart of Merrion Square, is a brilliant stop for families. Entry is free, and kids will love wandering through the bright galleries filled with colourful paintings and fascinating portraits. The gallery also runs creative workshops and family-friendly tours, making art feel accessible and fun for all ages. The Early Years, Children and Families Programme plays a major part in encouraging learning through art at the National Gallery of Ireland. This programme is for all the family, from babies and toddlers to younger teens and their parents/guardians, embracing play, enquiry, problem solving, independent thinking and collaboration through multi-disciplinary sensory activities responding to the national collection. The programme includes general activities on site for everyone, from seasoned museum visitors to tourist families on holiday. The gallery offers drop-in workshops, tours and activities, all with a family focus. The Atrium Creative Space is open every day, and free art packs are available. There are also scheduled workshops and events, designed with age-appropriate learning in mind. The gallery is currently developing an online strand for Early Years, Children and Families, which aims to take learning through art out of the gallery and into the home. After your visit, you can pop across to Merrion Square Park for a run-around - the perfect mix of culture and playtime.

The PlayLabs are weekly developmental sensory play classes for babies, wobblers and/or toddlers. The classes are designed to provide a fun and engaging environment for both children and adults! With music, learning, social interaction, and sensory play, you and your little one are sure to have a great time together. They are also a chance for children to socialise with children of their own age, while offering parents/caregivers the opportunity to get to know others in their area too. Baby PlayLabs (6 weeks - 7 months) - 55 minute class duration Wobbler PlayLabs (8 months - 15 months) - 50 minute class duration Toddler PlayLabs (16 months - 3.5 years) - 50 minute class duration We typically run toddlers at 9.45am/10am, wobblers at 10.45am/11am and babies at 11.45am/12pm - venue dependent. You can check out more information about our classes on our website: https://site.claphandies.com/ In addition to your term's booking, we also offer you 3 months free access to ClapHandies at Home which is an online resource filled with hours of follow-along music and guided playtime videos for you and your little ones to enjoy at home. So if you miss any of your classes during a term, you can run your own PlayLab at home or just tune in to have some extra fun during the week. If you are signing up for our Baby PlayLabs in particular, we also provide you with a link to our informative Baby Massage e-manual so you can continue practising the massage strokes at home.
