Elaines Gift of Touch

This space offers nurturing support for families through pregnancy massage, infant massage classes, parent and child massage classes, postpartum doula care, and Mum’s Haven support circles for expectant and new mums. Classes include infant massage for parents and babies, dad and baby massage classes, and parent–child massage classes for ages 2–3 and 4–12 years through the Massage in Schools Programme (MISP). These sessions encourage bonding, relaxation, emotional regulation, and positive touch in a safe, respectful environment. Sessions take place locally, with times and locations shared when booking or advertised for upcoming groups. Caregivers are warmly welcome to attend, including mums, dads, grandparents, or guardians. A caregiver must stay for the full duration of the class as these are shared connection sessions between adult and child. Children should wear comfortable clothing, and parents may wish to bring a small blanket or mat for baby massage classes. Massage oil and guidance are provided. Mum’s Haven circles offer a gentle, supportive space for expectant and new mothers to rest, connect, and feel supported within the community.
