Baby Sensory Class - Midwife For You

A calm and nurturing 4-week Gentle Sensory Class designed to gently stimulate your baby’s developing senses through lights, textures, sounds and movement in a relaxed environment. This class is thoughtfully designed by a registered midwife to provide gentle sensory experiences that support baby’s development while keeping stimulation calm and baby-led. It works beautifully as a follow-on from an Infant Massage Course, but can also be enjoyed as a standalone class for parents and babies. Sessions are relaxed and baby-led, with plenty of time for feeding, changing and settling babies as needed, as well as the opportunity to connect with other parents. Time & Location Classes take place in my private studio in Ballyhyland, Co. Laois. Who can attend Suitable for babies from 3 months to pre crawling. Mums, dads or caregivers such as grandparents are very welcome to attend. Do caregivers stay? Yes, caregivers stay for the duration of the class and take part with their baby. What to bring / wear Babies should wear comfortable clothing. No special equipment is required. Classes are facilitated by a registered midwife, creating a supportive and gentle space for both baby and caregiver.

Parents of these incredible children have come together to advocate on their behalf to ensure that these often forgotten children get every opportunity at inclusion and education in their local area. It is a great place to meet other parents going through the same challenges and a great place to see the kids make friends. The group is for kids aged 3 years to teens.
