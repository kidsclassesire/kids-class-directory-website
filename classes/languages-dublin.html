Stay at home Irish Summer College

Looking for a fun way to boost your child's Irish this summer? SárGhael is a South Dublin-based Irish summer college offering courses for 1st & 2nd years in IADT, and courses for 3rd-6th class in Clonkeen College. We focus on real Irish in action, helping students build confidence through speaking, sports, music, and making memories with friends - showing them that Irish isn't just for the classroom, it's a living, vibrant language! Alliance Française

Alliance Française Dublin is a leading French language and cultural centre offering classes for toddlers, children, and teenagers (ages 2-18). Through fun and interactive activities, students build lasting language skills and an appreciation for French culture. Located in the heart of Dublin, the centre is easily accessible and offers a welcoming space for members to relax and socialise. Recognised for excellence, it has received the European Language Label and was named Best Language School in Ireland in 2018.

Instituto Cervantes

Instituto Cervantes Dublin offers Spanish language courses for children and teenagers, helping them develop valuable language skills from an early age. Learning Spanish boosts educational success and future career prospects, as it's the second most widely used business language globally. Highly qualified native teachers use dynamic, communicative methods to make learning engaging and effective. Located in the heart of Dublin at Lincoln House, the school also offers access to an extensive library and cultural activities to enhance the learning experience.
