Avoca Hockey Club

Dublin

Avoca Hockey Club, based in South Dublin, is one of Ireland's oldest and largest hockey clubs, with a vibrant junior section of around 800 boys and girls training each week. The club offers a fun, inclusive, and safe environment where children of all levels can develop their skills, make friends, and enjoy the sport, whether they're playing for fun or aspiring to compete. Junior training takes place across several South Dublin locations, including Newpark Comprehensive School, Sion Hill, Loreto Foxrock, and St Andrew's College, ensuring access for families throughout the area. Affiliated with Hockey Ireland and the Leinster Hockey Association, Avoca's junior section is a great entry point into the sport, supported by experienced coaches and a strong community spirit.

DMcG Hockey

DMcG Hockey runs specialised hockey camps and skills clinics in Dublin for young players who want to improve their technical game. Led by expert coaches, many with Leinster and Irish international experience, each session is tailored to help players develop core hockey skills and tactical understanding. DMcG focuses on individual player development, enhancing stick work, control, and game sense through targeted drills and game-specific problem-solving - the goal is for every player to have fun, build confidence, and leave better than they arrived. Many participants have gone on to represent Leinster and Ireland at underage levels. Camps are open to players of all levels who are eager to grow their game.

Genesis Hockey Club

Dublin

Genesis Juniors is a thriving part of Genesis Hockey Club, welcoming girls from 1st class through to Under 16s. With over 380 junior members and 24 dedicated coaches, the club provides a safe, fun, and supportive environment for young players to learn, play, and grow through hockey. Training takes place on Sunday mornings for primary and secondary school groups at Mount Anville School, with U16 teams training and playing matches on Sunday afternoons at St Raphaela's School, Kilmacud. Beyond hockey, the club offers a vibrant social scene for juniors, with cinema outings, fun days, and trips that help foster friendships and team spirit.

Monkstown Hockey Club

Dublin

Monkstown Hockey Club's Academy is home to over 650 young players, with around 200 boys and 450 girls, aged 6 to 16. With a strong focus on fun, skill development, and team spirit, the Academy has been a core part of the club for over 50 years, creating a pathway from junior hockey to senior success. Training sessions are held across multiple pitches, and teams take part in regular matches and blitzes, following the IHA's Hooked for Life model. The Academy prioritises small-group coaching, player progression, and, most importantly, a safe and enjoyable environment.

Muckross Hockey Club

Dublin

Muckross Hockey Club's thriving Junior Section welcomes girls from 1st class to 4th year, offering a fun, inclusive introduction to hockey built on friendship, teamwork, and community spirit.
