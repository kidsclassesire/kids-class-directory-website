Taekwon-Do for Kids Ages 6-12

Sports

Kids Taekwon-Do based in St Joseph's Primary School, Fairview, Dublin. Mondays & Wednesdays, 4:30-5:30pm. Great for building confidence, discipline & focus, physical fitness, and making friends. Tolka TKD encourages a friendly environment where children can express themselves and join a great team. The lead instructor is Lee Kavanagh (3rd Dan Black Belt), who is also a primary school teacher, has represented Team Ireland at World and European Championships, and has 17+ years' experience. The club is a member of the Irish Taekwon-Do Association (ITA), the National Association for the International Taekwon-Do Federation (ITF) in Ireland.
