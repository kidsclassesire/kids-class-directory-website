Little Puckers - GAA class for preschoolers in Cork

GAA

Join Little Puckers in creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for all children to learn and grow through the joy of Gaelic Games (GAA). Little Puckers is a fun and inclusive Gaelic Games-based programme for children aged 1.5-5 years old, committed to providing easy access to hurling and football for every child. Parents are key stakeholders in their child's enjoyment at Little Puckers - you'll be asked to play with your child for most of the session, and these collaborative activities help develop strong bonds with your child, leading to a stronger relationship. All equipment is provided at the classes, so there's no need to bring anything except a drink. 9 locations in Cork: Blarney (Scoil Mhuire Gan Smal), Bandon (Bandon Grammar School), Macroom (Macroom Community Centre), Kinsale (Saile Community Centre), Carrigaline (Carrigaline Community Complex), Ballinlough (St Anthony's BNS), Midleton (Midleton Educate Together), Fermoy (Gaelscoil de hÍde), and Glanmire (Upper Glanmire Community Hall).
