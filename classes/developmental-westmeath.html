Lasting Touch Baby Massage

Baby massage classes in Athlone, Ballinasloe and Craughwell, facilitated by a registered midwife/certified infant massage instructor. Ballinasloe classes: every Thursday, 18th June-16th July, 10-11.30am. Athlone classes: every Friday, 19th June-17th July, 10-11.30am. Craughwell classes: every Wednesday, 1st-29th July, 10-11.30am. Up to €100 redeemable with health insurers. Tea/coffee and a sweet treat provided - a lovely opportunity to bond with your baby and meet other mums in the area. Group classes are limited to one parent and one baby per booking. Private classes are available in your own home if additional parents or children would like to be present. Contact Emily for more info and to book. Instagram: @lastingtouch_babymassge. Email: lastingtouchbabymassage@gmail.com

Bloom Baby Classes - Midlands

Classes are held on various days and times in multiple locations - see website for more information. Caterpillar Club is an introductory baby class for babies 0-6 months. Watch your baby gaze in delight at our bespoke, bright props, with a different theme each week to treasure memories from baby's first, most important year of life. Each baby class ends in a gentle baby massage to relax baby and prepare them for a nice long nap. Busy Bees: at theme time, your baby gets to explore our fun range of bright props and exciting heuristic treasure baskets while developing social skills with fellow Bloom babies - suitable for babies from 6 months to walking, or 15 months. Bloom Toddler Classes: with our own bespoke music, including a phonics song, numbers song and calming zen track, our back-to-back activities for toddlers aid development and focus on first early learning goals.
