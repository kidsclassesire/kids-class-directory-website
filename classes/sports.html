International volleyball Ireland

International Volleyball Ireland (IVI) is one of Ireland's fastest-growing volleyball communities, bringing together players of all backgrounds, nationalities, and skill levels. Offerings include weekly volleyball training sessions for beginners, intermediate and advanced players, junior volleyball programmes for children and teenagers, National League teams for competitive players, social volleyball sessions and tournaments throughout the year, and coaching and player development opportunities, all within a welcoming and inclusive environment. Whether you're completely new to volleyball or an experienced player looking for a team, IVI provides opportunities to learn, improve, compete, and make new friends through sport. Based in Dublin, and open to players of all nationalities and backgrounds.
