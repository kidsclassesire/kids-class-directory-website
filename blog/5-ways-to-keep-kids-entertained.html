5 Foolproof Ways to Keep Your Kids Entertained (and Learning!) This Weekend

By Kids Patch Team · 08 August 2026

We've all been there. It's Saturday morning, the coffee hasn't quite kicked in, and the dreaded two words echo from the hallway: "I'm bored!"

Finding engaging things to do with kids that don't involve staring at a screen can feel like a full-time job. Whether you're looking for last-minute family day out ideas, indoor rainy day activities, or wondering if it's time to book a new after-school class, we've got you covered.

Here are five tried-and-true ways to keep the little ones busy, happy, and learning this weekend.

1. Create a "Boredom Buster" Jar

Sometimes the hardest part of finding kids' activities is just making the decision. Sit down with your children and write out 20 simple activities on popsicle sticks or slips of paper.

Mix high-energy ideas with quiet-time tasks. When boredom strikes, they pull a stick and have to do what it says!

Examples: Build a blanket fort, draw a portrait of the family pet, do 15 jumping jacks, or see who can build the tallest Lego tower.

2. Embrace the Weather (Yes, Even the Rain)

There's no such thing as bad weather, only bad clothing! One of the best free things to do with kids is to simply get them outside. Nature play is incredible for sensory development and burning off excess energy.

  • Dry days: Have a scavenger hunt. Make a list of items to find at your local park (a brown leaf, a smooth rock, a feather).
  • Wet days: Put on the wellies and raincoats and have a puddle-jumping competition.

3. Book a Term Class to Build Independence

If you find yourself constantly scrambling for weekend activities, the easiest solution is to lock in a recurring class. Not only does it give your weekend structure, but it also helps children socialize, build confidence, and discover new passions.

Unsure what to book? Here's a quick guide to matching classes to your child's personality:

If your child loves to... Try searching for... Key Benefits
Move constantly Gymnastics, Dance, Martial Arts Gross motor skills, discipline, coordination
Ask "how does it work?" Coding, STEM, Lego Robotics Problem-solving, logic, patience
Make a mess Pottery, Art, Messy Play Sensory exploration, creativity, focus
Perform for the family Drama, Musical Theatre, Singing Confidence building, public speaking, teamwork

Tip: You can filter by age and interest right here on Kids Patch to find the perfect fit near you.

4. The "Kitchen Science" Afternoon

You don't need a lab coat to blow their minds. Kitchen science experiments use things you already have in the cupboard and double as incredible sensory activities for toddlers and older kids alike.

  • The Classic Volcano: Baking soda, vinegar, and a drop of food coloring.
  • Oobleck: Mix 2 parts cornstarch with 1 part water. Is it a solid? Is it a liquid? It will keep them occupied for ages while you enjoy a hot cup of tea.

5. Lean into Boredom

Here's a secret: it's actually okay for kids to be bored. You don't have to be a cruise ship entertainment director 24/7. When children are left to their own devices without structured activities or screens, their imaginations are forced to take over.

The next time they complain about having nothing to do, tell them it's a great opportunity to invent a new game. You might be surprised at the worlds they build in the living room.
